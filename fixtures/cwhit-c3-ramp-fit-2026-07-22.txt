### THE DECLARED FORM (declared before the numbers, per pre-registration §3)

    s(g) = 1 + A·(g/20)^q      for g > 0;      s(g ≤ 0) = 1 EXACTLY

  · G0 = 20 is a FIXED reference gap (mid observed range), not a parameter: it only fixes the
    units of A, so A = s(20) − 1 reads directly off the constant.
  · TWO fitted parameters: A (amplitude) and q (curvature). q > 1 CONVEX, q = 1 the LINEAR
    LIMIT, q < 1 concave/saturating. Monotone increasing for A > 0, q > 0.
  · s(0) = 1 is exact by construction — the league anchor, not a fitted quantity.
  · WHY NOT THE OLD FAMILY: 1 + A(1 − e^(−g/G)) is concave for EVERY (A,G) and was falsified for
    being unable to bend convexly. The power law is the minimal one-extra-parameter monotone
    family that contains the linear limit as an INTERIOR point and can bend either way.
  · OBJECTIVE: per-card residuals weighted by per-card noise, w_i = 1/per9NoiseVar(obs_i, BF_i).
    corrected_i = K̄_t + s(g_t)·(K_i − K̄_t), K̄_t = poolPitMeansOwn(presenceMixture(pool)).k — the
    same pivot and the same presence-mixture population production centres on. NO tier aggregate
    enters the objective. NO per-tier freedom: one function of gap, five tiers, quicks only.
  · PINNING RULE (pre-registered, unchanged in form from BUILD-1): the most-saturating member
    (smallest q) whose SSE is within 5% of the LINEAR-LIMIT SSE, reported with the band and the
    linear limit. Cross-fit comparisons are made on s(g) over the observed gap range, NEVER on
    raw {A, q} — those are two pinning outcomes. Grid q ∈ [0.05, 8.00] step 0.01, A closed-form at each q.

### 3. ACCEPTANCE B — THE 1000 BAR IS A SENSITIVITY REPORT, NOT A GATE

  STRUCTURAL REASON, stated up front: a POOL-LEVEL ramp is one function s(g) of a FORMAT'S GAP.
  The gap is a property of the eligible pool — a catalog property — and is computed identically
  whatever usage floor the EVALUATION set is drawn at. So s(g_t) returns the SAME scalar at the
  600 bar and the 1000 bar: it CANNOT move between bars, and demanding it reproduce a bar
  differential would be demanding the impossible. Gold's bar differential stays a DIAGNOSTIC,
  attached to the five-card provenance, and is never scored as a pass or a fail.
