## VERDICT

  THE CANDIDATE PUT FORWARD, AND WHAT HAPPENED TO IT: CHANNEL DECOUPLING — the signed divergence
  between the ONE opposing channel a correction's gap coordinate reads and the pool's COMPOSITE
  gap position over its whole rating vector. For BABIP that is `decoup_babip` = z(hitter-BABIP
  gap) − mean over all five hitter channels of their standardized gaps. Continuous, ex ante,
  from eligible-pool ratings + the artifact's training means only — no card type, no pool
  identity, no value window, and one channel measured against a composite rather than against
  another single rating.

  ANSWER TO THE HEADLINE QUESTION: NO PROPERTY IN THIS CLASS ORDERS THE NEED, BECAUSE ON TODAY'S
  SHIPPED LINE THE BABIP NEED HAS NO MEASURABLE HETEROGENEITY TO ORDER.
  · Five-Quick baseline: needs span 0.91–1.38, but the INTERSECTION of all five 95% CIs is
    [1.05, 1.41] — NON-EMPTY. A ZERO-parameter constant (1.24 ± 0.09) passes through every one.
    Q = 2.96 on 4 df, I2 = 0%, p = 0.567. Adding the high-divergence point, then
    stratum B, then stratum C never empties the intersection (1.13–1.30 at all 14).
  · THIS IS NOT A POWER FAILURE, AND THE RUN PROVES IT WITH A POSITIVE CONTROL. The SAME
    instrument, same formats, same bar, same card-clustered bootstrap, pointed at the K channel
    on the same shipped line: Q = 51.96 on 4 df, I2 = 92%, p = 0.000, CI intersection EMPTY.
    The method detects real between-format heterogeneity in this corpus at these sample sizes. It
    finds none in BABIP.

  MECHANISTIC OR COINCIDENTAL: THE MECHANISM IS ESTABLISHED, THE ORDERING IS COINCIDENTAL.
  · The mechanism is not speculative. buildFrameShift builds the BABIP coordinate exactly as it
    builds the K coordinate — pit.pbabip's shift IS train.hit.babip − pool.hit.babip, one
    opposing channel read alone (src/scoring-core/pool-stats.ts:236,242-244). Whatever made the K
    coordinate misread a rating-SHAPE difference as a level difference is structurally present in
    the BABIP coordinate. That is a code fact, not an inference from fourteen samples.
  · The decoupling is real and it is largest where the K story says: |decoup_kRat| runs
    0.011–0.657 across the corpus; within the clean stratum the largest is liveopendaily at
    0.471, 2.2x the Quick-baseline mean, and it is the ONLY format in the clean stratum whose
    avoid-K channel sits AHEAD of its composite by more than 0.1 (+0.471 vs -0.55, -0.29, -0.13, +0.01, +0.09).
    (Corpus-wide the largest |decoup_kRat| is an env-modified format, which is why the clean
    stratum is where the statement is made.) That measured number — not what kind of cards the
    format admits — is the ground for treating it as a divergence point rather than a baseline.
  · But its ORDERING of the BABIP need is Spearman 0.486, and propagating the needs' own
    card-clustered uncertainty gives [-0.37, 0.83] — a CI that spans zero and most of the
    negative range. There is no ordering evidence here in either direction.
  · The family's BEST ordering is predbab_sd at Spearman -1.000 (permutation p = 0.043 for the
    family maximum). DO NOT READ THAT AS A FINDING. Its own bootstrap CI is [-1.00, 0.03]: it orders
    perfectly a set of six numbers that are statistically indistinguishable from six copies of
    one constant. A perfect ordering of noise is still an ordering of noise, and the permutation
    test — which permutes the point estimates and cannot see their CIs — is blind to that.
    (It is also the least surprising candidate imaginable: a spread correction's need is the
    ratio of true spread to PREDICTED spread, so the predicted spread is its own denominator.)

## 2. CHANNEL DECOUPLING — the construction, and what it measures

  For each channel c of the OPPOSING role's rating vector:
      z_c        = (trainingMean_c - poolCohortMean_c) / catalogSD_c
      composite  = mean over ALL FIVE hitter channels of z_c        <- the whole rating vector
      decoup_c   = z_c - composite                                  <- one channel vs the composite
  The composite is the point of the construction: contrasting ONE channel against a COMPOSITE is
  what makes this a decoupling. If both sides were single ratings it would be a correlation
  between two ratings and would rebuild the very defect it exists to escape.

## 4. THE ANALOGY, ASKED DIRECTLY

      YES, and this is a code fact. src/scoring-core/pool-stats.ts buildFrameShift:
        pit.stu    = train.hit.kRat  - pool.hit.kRat     <- the K ramp's gap
        pit.pbabip = train.hit.babip - pool.hit.babip    <- the BABIP need's gap

## 10. WHAT EACH CANDIDATE IS, AND WHETHER IT READS ONE CHANNEL OR A COMPOSITE

  decoup_babip         LEAD. z(hitter BABIP gap) − composite: how far the ONE channel the BABIP correction reads sits from the pool's whole-rating-vector gap position. Signed; >0 = that channel's gap runs ahead of the composite.
  decoup_babip_abs     |decoup_babip| — divergence magnitude, direction-free.
  decoup_kRat          the SAME construction for the channel the K ramp reads (hitter avoid-K). Present so the two channels' decoupling can be compared on one scale.
  decoup_kRat_abs      |decoup_kRat|.
  shape_div_hit        SD across ALL FIVE hitter channels of the standardized gaps — how far this pool's gap VECTOR departs from a uniform shift. Channel-agnostic; a pure shape statistic.
  composite_hit        COMPOSITE gap position: mean over all five hitter channels of the standardized (train − pool) gap. The whole rating vector, never one rating standing in for quality.
  composite_pit        the same composite over the four pitcher channels.
  shape_div_pit        SD across the four pitcher channels of the standardized gaps.
  decoup_pbabip_own    z(pitcher pBABIP gap) − composite_pit — the own-side analogue, for completeness.
  gap_babip_channel    INCUMBENT for BABIP. buildFrameShift's pit.pbabip = train.hit.babip − pool.hit.babip, i.e. the OPPOSING-HITTER BABIP rating gap READ ALONE. Single-channel by construction — the same shape as the K coordinate.
  gap_kRat_channel     the K ramp's coordinate = train.hit.kRat − pool.hit.kRat, opposing avoid-K READ ALONE.
  gap_pow_channel      the HR ramp's coordinate = train.hit.pow − pool.hit.pow.
  gap_eye_channel      the control channel's coordinate = train.hit.eye − pool.hit.eye.
  gap_pit_pbabip       buildFrameShift's hit.babip = train.pit.pbabip − pool.pit.pbabip (the pitcher-side pBABIP gap; it re-bases HITTERS, listed for completeness).
  pbab_mu              mean pBABIP rating of the eligible pitchers (a level on one channel — reported, never a quality claim).
  pbab_sd              SD of the eligible pitchers' pBABIP rating — the spread a spread-scalar would act on.
  pbab_skew            skew of that distribution.
  pbab_p90_p10         its interdecile range.
  hbab_mu              mean BABIP rating of the eligible hitters.
  hbab_sd              SD of the eligible hitters' BABIP rating.
  predbab_mu           pool-mean PREDICTED pitcher BABIP on the own-gap line — the pivot a spread scalar uses.
  predbab_sd           pool SD of predicted pitcher BABIP — the predicted spread a scalar rescales.
  predbab_cv           predicted-BABIP coefficient of variation.
  bip_mu               pool-mean predicted BIP per 600 on the SHIPPED line. BABIP is a rate on balls in play and BIP is DERIVED as 600 − BB − K − HR, so a pool with a different channel MIX has a different BIP base — which no single-channel gap can see.
  bip_sd               pool SD of predicted BIP per 600.
  k_mu                 pool-mean predicted K per 600 on the shipped line (post K-ramp).
  bb_mu                pool-mean predicted BB per 600.
  hr_mu                pool-mean predicted HR per 600 on the shipped line (post HR-ramp).
  hitspread_600        SD across the pool of (predicted BABIP × predicted BIP) — the channel's spread in EVENT (hits per 600) space rather than rate space.
  valueMax             the format's card-value ceiling — THE TIER INDEX ITSELF. A pure identity control, present so anything tracking it is caught.
  windowWidth          valueMax − valueMin (VAL 40 is the game's floor).
  poolN_pit            eligible pitcher count.
  capTight             budget tightness = 1 − total_cap / cost of the most expensive legal roster (tools/cap-tightness.ts, the one definition). 0 uncapped.
  forcedCheap          share of a legal roster that must sit below the pool's own affordability line.
  era_h                era HIT factor VALUE (derived) — environment axis, diagnostic only.
  era_k                era K factor VALUE — environment axis, diagnostic only.
  park_avg             park average-hits factor, hand-averaged (compressed, cp 0.26) — environment axis, diagnostic only.
  s_K                  the shipped K-ramp scalar at this format's gap.
  s_HR                 the shipped HR-ramp scalar at this format's gap.

  SINGLE-CHANNEL EXPOSURE, stated per family — the defect being diagnosed is single-channel
  reading, so every candidate must declare whether it is exposed to it:
   · DECOUPLING (decoup_*, shape_div_*, composite_*) — reads ONE channel AGAINST A COMPOSITE over
     the whole rating vector. NOT exposed: a pool that moves uniformly on every channel has
     decoup = 0 by construction, so no uniform level difference can be misread as shape.
   · SINGLE-CHANNEL GAPS (gap_*_channel) — reads ONE channel, full stop. FULLY EXPOSED. This is
     the incumbent BABIP coordinate, and it is the K coordinate's known failure mode verbatim:
     a pool whose rating vector is ahead of the frame on four channels and behind on the fifth
     reads, through a single-channel gap, exactly like a pool that is behind on everything.
   · OWN-POOL RATING SHAPE (pbab_*, hbab_*) — single-channel levels/spreads. EXPOSED, and on this
     corpus additionally indistinguishable from the value window.
   · BIP VOLUME (bip_*, k_mu, bb_mu, hr_mu, hitspread_600) — derived from THREE channels at once
     (BB, K, HR) plus the BABIP rate. PARTLY protected: it cannot be moved by one channel alone,
     but it is not composite-referenced either, so a coordinated shift in all three still moves it.
   · WINDOW/SIZE (valueMax, windowWidth, poolN_pit) — NO mechanism. Controls only.
   · BUDGET (capTight, forcedCheap) — a real mechanism on AXIS 2, and the baseline contains no
     budget variation at all, so it is unmeasurable there by construction.
   · ENVIRONMENT (era_*, park_avg) — a real mechanism on AXIS 1. Excluded from the baseline ranking.

## 11. WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT (design, not statistics)

  The binding constraint is DESIGN, not statistics. Inside the five-Quick baseline nearly every
  candidate — the decoupling family included (§6) — reads |rho| ~ 1 against the value window,
  because the windows are nested. The corpus cannot separate a pool property from the value
  window even in principle. Three things break that, in order of value:
   (a) NEUTRAL, NON-BUDGET formats with NON-NESTED windows — a floor-bearing window (valueMin > 40)
       on era-2010/park-1 gives a pool whose rating shape is not a prefix of another's.
       `high-iron-floor-gold-ceiling` (50-89) has exactly this shape but is capped, so it lands in
       B+C; an UNCAPPED min-bearing neutral format is the ask.
   (b) FORMATS SPREAD ALONG THE DECOUPLING AXIS. Today the axis has a dense cluster and one
       extreme point (|decoup_kRat| 0.214 baseline mean vs 0.471). One point cannot
       identify a response. Intermediate-divergence formats are the discriminating capture, and
       they should be selected BY THEIR COMPUTED DIVERGENCE, which is available ex ante for all 48
       configured tournaments without any new data.
   (c) DEPTH. Baseline need-SEs are 0.16-0.37. Separating a coordinate from a constant needs
       the CI intersection to go EMPTY, which at these spreads needs roughly a halving of the SEs,
       i.e. about 4x the judged rows per format. The 4-week pull is the natural instrument.
