# READING — the answer is MIXED, and the two framings disagree on purpose
################################################################################

THE PRE-REGISTERED BRANCH was: insensitive across the plausible p band ⇒ a global p ships;
sensitive ⇒ a property-conditioned p comes back as a proposal. The measurement does not land
cleanly on either side, and the reason is worth stating rather than rounding away.

AS A SHARE OF EACH TIER'S OWN CORRECTION, sensitivity rises monotonically and reaches a level
that would matter:  iron 1.5% · bronze 4.0% · silver 7.5% · gold 11.0% · diamond 17.0%.

IN ABSOLUTE TERMS THE SWING IS SMALL AND NEARLY FLAT:  0.011 · 0.028 · 0.048 · 0.056 · 0.057
in s_K. The percentage grows almost entirely because the DENOMINATOR shrinks — the correction
itself falls from 0.731 at iron to 0.335 at diamond — not because the choice of p bites
harder at the top tiers.

Which framing governs is a decision, not a measurement, and it is the difference between
"a global p is fine" and "diamond needs its own". Both numbers are above so neither can be
quoted without the other.

TWO THINGS WORTH CARRYING BEYOND THE HEADLINE:

· THE GAP IS NOT MONOTONE IN p. Iron runs 23.64 / 21.22 / 22.15 / 21.59 / 19.06 across
  p = 0 / 0.2 / 0.3 / 0.4 / 1.0, and diamond RISES before falling (10.38 / 10.89 / 10.18 /
  9.06 / 7.04). A pure re-basing would be monotone; this is selection churn — which cards win
  top-50 seats changes non-smoothly as the mixture weight moves. It means p cannot be reasoned
  about by interpolating between the endpoints, and any fitted ramp must be re-derived at the
  chosen p rather than scaled from p = 0.

· p DOES NOT RESCUE DIAMOND. Its measured need is 1.04; s_K runs 1.335 at p = 0 and 1.293 at
  p = 0.4. Production over-corrects diamond at every p on this sweep, so the standing diamond
  G1 failure is not a variant-policy artefact and will not be fixed by choosing p well. That
  also retires the last remnant of the "diamond becomes in-frame" expectation: it depended on
  the ungated arm-C construction, which is refuted, and it does not survive eligibility gating.

METHOD NOTE. The mixture is exact, not approximated: for rational p = k/m each ineligible card
is emitted m times and each eligible card as (m-k) base copies plus k v5 copies, with top-N
scaled to N x m. Duplicates carry identical predicted value, so the top-(N·m) of the replicated
population IS the weighted top-N of the mixture and its moments ARE the weighted moments. The
shared core computes all of it — no weighted-moment variant was written, which would have been
a second copy of scoring maths.
