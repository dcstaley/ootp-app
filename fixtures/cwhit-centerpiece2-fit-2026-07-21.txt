################################################################################
# READING — the direction was right, the LOCALIZATION is wrong, and that is fatal
################################################################################

INSTRUMENT FIRST. The tool-side per-side reconstruction matches the builder's own raw K9
line to max |diff| = 0.00e+0 across every row, so the correction is being applied to the
deployed curve and not to a lookalike. The re-measured needs reproduce the committed
values exactly at both bars (1.82/1.62/1.48/1.78/1.04 and 1.83/1.64/1.47/1.63/0.85), and
they barely move under arm C (1.81/1.62/1.47/1.77/1.04) — confirming the ruling's premise
that the per-tier slopes are coordinate-free targets.

THE KILL CONDITION FIRES FIRST, AND IT FIRES UNDER BOTH POLICIES.

  A1.5(7): the knot fits at r0 = 121.00 — EXACTLY the league training median, which is the
  upper edge of its own constrained grid. Boundary-pinned under both the current coordinate
  and arm C's. By the standing rule a boundary-pinned optimum is a family misfit; by the
  registered kill condition a knot at the training median is "a global slope wearing a
  hinge". The data does not want a correction localized at low support. It wants one that
  acts everywhere it is allowed to act, and it pushes the knot as high as the constraint
  permits. That is precisely the shape step 0 ruled inadmissible.

EVERY PRIMARY GATE FAILS, INDEPENDENTLY.

  P1 (>=4 of 5 inside CI @600): 3 of 5 on the current coordinate, 1 of 5 under arm C. FAIL.
  P2 (held-out gold, BOTH bars): @600 implied 1.770 vs measured 1.783 [1.64,1.90] INSIDE;
     @1000 implied 1.761 vs measured 1.634 [1.50,1.74] OUTSIDE. Same pattern under arm C
     (1.832 inside, 1.810 outside). FAIL — and the failure is diagnostic: held-out gold is
     reproduced at the bar where gold looks anomalous and missed at the bar where it does
     not, because the function cannot move between bars at all.
  P3 (the differential): gold implied -0.010 against measured -0.149 on the current
     coordinate; -0.024 against -0.142 under arm C. The function reproduces 7-17% of the
     one movement that distinguishes gold. FAIL.

  THE SINGLE CLEAREST NUMBER IS DIAMOND: implied 1.60 against a measured need of 1.04. A
  hinge knotted at the median produces a near-uniform slope of 1.6-1.8 at EVERY tier, so it
  cannot deliver a tier that needs ~1.0 while another needs ~1.8. Producing five different
  local slopes was the whole test.

THE COORDINATE IS NOT DISTINGUISHED — the pre-registered N2 finding, stated rather than
buried. The same hinge on PREDICTED K rather than on a rating scores weighted SSE 2737.9
against the primary's 2729.9 on the current coordinate (0.3% apart), and under arm C it is
outright BETTER: 3168.8 against 3462.6. So the data does not identify a RATING-LOCAL error
at all; what it identifies is curvature in the prediction-to-observation mapping, which a
pure calibration coordinate captures at least as well. The "rating-local" clause of the
hypothesis has no support in this measurement.

WHAT DOES SURVIVE, and it is not nothing:

  · THE SIGN IS AS PREDICTED, everywhere: A < 0 in all six fits (three coordinates x two
    policies). The model over-predicts K where the correction bites, exactly as the five
    named sub-p05 cards in the gold query showed at card grain.
  · THE CONSTANT NULL LOSES BADLY: N1 c = 0.909 at weighted SSE 7334 against the hinge's
    2730. There IS a real, large, systematic curvature in the K channel. It is simply not
    localized, and not rating-indexed.
  · Taken together those say the residual is a broad spread/calibration defect in K — much
    closer in character to the original spread-scalar story than to a curve error confined
    to a thin, poorly-supported tail.

NO POLICY FLIP. Every gate fails the same way under both coordinates, so ruling (h)'s
STOP-on-flip condition is not triggered; this is one consistent refutation rather than a
coordinate-dependent artefact. Arm C is uniformly slightly worse for this family, which is
a fact about this family and NOT an argument against arm C — arm C's case rests on the
invariant and on diamond's in-frame gain, neither of which this measurement touches.

WHAT THIS COSTS AND WHAT IT LEAVES. The rating-local extrapolation hypothesis — the prime
candidate since the battery — is refuted on its own pre-registered terms, under both
policies, on three independent gates plus a kill condition. Gold's residual break is now
unexplained by: the opponent field, opponent dispersion, card design, new releases, and
rating-local curve error. What remains on the table is the five-card low-support tail as a
DESCRIPTION (it is where the variance sits) without a mechanism that reproduces the needs,
and the parked interaction hypothesis that this corpus structurally cannot test.
