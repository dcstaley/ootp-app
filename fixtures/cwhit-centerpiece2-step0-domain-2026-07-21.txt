################################################################################
# READING — the pre-registered trichotomy does not cover what was found
################################################################################

The prereg committed to three readings: cards OUTSIDE the fitted domain (literal
extrapolation live), cards INSIDE but in the sparse low tail (weaker rating-local form),
or cards in the WELL-SUPPORTED INTERIOR (stop, for BUILD-2's reason). The measurement
returns the first and third simultaneously, at different points of the same distribution,
so the reading is escalated rather than chosen.

1. LITERAL EXTRAPOLATION IS DEAD. Only 2.6-6.3% of realized cards fall below uMin, and
   diamond's realized set is 0.0% below it. The own-gap lift does carry tournament cards
   back inside the span the curve was fitted over. The tangent-linear extension is not
   the mechanism.

2. THE TYPICAL CARD IS WELL SUPPORTED. Realized median effective stuff sits at league
   training percentile 27.8 / 53.1 / 31.4 / 59.5 / 47.0 (iron -> diamond). The median
   gold pitcher sits ABOVE the league training median. On the median card, this is
   BUILD-2's situation exactly, and BUILD-2's structural argument would apply.

3. BUT THE VARIANCE DOES NOT LIVE AT THE MEDIAN. A calibration slope is variance-
   weighted — a card's influence goes as (pred - mean)^2 — and the thinly-supported low
   region carries far more leverage than its card count suggests:

     tier      cards below train p05     share of predicted-K VARIANCE     measured need
     iron       22 of 142  (15.5%)                41.2%                        1.82
     bronze     13 of 156   (8.3%)                30.7%                        1.62
     silver     16 of 121  (13.2%)                34.2%                        1.48
     GOLD       11 of  91  (12.1%)                50.8%                        1.78
     diamond     2 of  36   (5.6%)                20.3%                        1.04

   ELEVEN GOLD CARDS CARRY HALF OF GOLD'S PREDICTED-K VARIANCE, and they sit below the
   5th percentile of the league training support. Diamond, the tier whose need is ~1.0,
   has the least leverage in that region (20.3%). The leverage ordering puts GOLD FIRST
   and DIAMOND LAST — Spearman ~0.8 against the needs, with gold, the tier that broke the
   gap curve, at the top.

   n = 5 nested tiers. This is a pattern to CARRY, not a result. It cannot separate a
   real support-leverage dependence from the mechanical fact that any pool's extreme low
   tail is both high-leverage and low-support.

4. WHAT THIS DOES TO THE HYPOTHESIS. It is not falsified and it is not confirmed. The
   version that survives is narrower and more testable than the one pre-registered: not
   "the curve is extrapolating", but "a small, thinly-supported, high-leverage low tail
   is where the slope deficiency would have to live". That is a sharper claim, because it
   predicts the need should be sensitive to exactly those cards.

5. AND IT COLLIDES WITH THE SAME DAY'S OTHER RESULT. The needs-vs-bar measurement
   (fixtures/cwhit-battery-needs-vs-bar-2026-07-21.txt) found gold's need falls 1.78 ->
   1.63 when lightly-used cards are dropped, while iron/bronze/silver hold. Gold is also
   the tier whose low-support tail carries the most variance. WHETHER THOSE ARE THE SAME
   ELEVEN CARDS IS NOT MEASURED HERE and is the single sharpest next question: if the
   high-leverage low-support cards are also the lightly-used ones, then support-leverage
   and selection-on-outcome are confounded at gold and neither reading stands alone.

CAVEAT ON THE SUPPORT COMPARISON. Tournament-side z is EFFECTIVE stuff (own-gap applied,
the argument the curve is evaluated at); league-side z is the raw training rating (the
argument it was fitted at). That is the correct pairing. The K curve is a 1-D function of
stuff alone, so the marginal density IS the right support measure for it — no joint-
support caveat is needed for this channel. League training support is thin in absolute
terms: 149 observations at BF>=1000, vL/vR counted separately.
