################################################################################
# VERDICT — the weighting matters a great deal, and it does NOT rescue K
################################################################################

MEAN ABSOLUTE ERROR vs the measured needs, uniform -> realized:
    K      0.469 -> 0.473    materially unchanged, marginally worse
    HR     1.405 -> 0.543    closer at EVERY tier; error falls to 39% of uniform
    BABIP  0.301 -> 0.188    closer overall; further at silver only

1. THE K MISS STANDS, AND IT IS THE CHANNEL THAT MATTERS. The candidate stays flat —
   1.091 / 1.086 / 1.087 / 1.073 / 1.043 against needs spanning 1.04 to 1.82. Four of five
   tiers move very slightly FURTHER away. Gold, the anomaly the whole property hunt exists
   for, sits at 1.073 against a need of 1.78. Re-weighting the opponents to the field
   actually faced does not begin to close that.

2. BUT THE WEIGHTING IS NOT A DETAIL. HR improves at every tier and its mean error drops
   to 39% of the uniform value; diamond lands at 1.277 against a need of 1.32. BABIP
   improves overall. So the eligible-pool assumption WAS materially wrong — item 4 measured
   it at 0.18-1.36 pool-SD and this is that error propagating into predicted spread. Two of
   three channels care.

3. THE CHANNEL-SELECTIVE PATTERN IS THE INFORMATIVE PART. A defect in the opponent
   distribution moves HR and BABIP substantially and K almost not at all. K's insensitivity
   to the opponent field is itself a finding: whatever drives the K need, it is not the
   composition of the opposing field, under either weighting.

4. HR STILL SLOPES THE WRONG WAY. Realized HR runs 2.200 -> 1.277, monotone FALLING, against
   a need of ~1.19 that is flat-to-slightly-rising. It is much closer in level and still
   wrong in shape.

WHAT THIS SETTLES ABOUT THE EARLIER MISS. The item-1 verdict was computed on eligible-uniform
opponents, and I flagged that it might therefore be a verdict on the wrong field. It is not,
for K: correcting the field leaves K essentially where it was. For HR and BABIP the original
numbers WERE materially distorted by the weighting and should not be quoted from the
eligible-uniform run.

LIMITATIONS
 · VLvl-5 VARIANTS ARE EXCLUDED — 785 of 3078 observed rows (25.5%), 104 hit + 73 pit at gold.
   Variants are runtime-generated and absent from the catalog CSV, so their usage cannot enter
   the weights at all. This is the largest hole in the realized figures.
 · Coverage is thin and concentrated: nonzero-weight opponents run 256/659 (iron hit) down to
   93/1534 (diamond pit), with the heaviest 10% holding 45-69% of usage.
 · The SCALAR CONTROL column relates to the UNIFORM integration only (it collapses opponents to
   their uniform mean). It is NOT the degenerate limit of the realized run, and the pinned
   identity in tests/battery-deficit.test.ts does not cover the realized path.
 · Realized usage is NOT ex-ante computable, so nothing here is a shippable coordinate; it is a
   diagnostic on whether the opponent field is the right one.
