HBP, THE LEG THAT IS DIFFERENT IN KIND — decision, justification, and the measurement both ways.
The form gives every hitter a FIXED 6/600 against an observed 5.15/600: 94% of the composite
LEVEL bias, and the real rate carries a CI-clear EYE slope no anchor can absorb. It is also the
leg that WORKS BEST on its own channel — the fitted constant lands at 4.98–5.16/600 across all
three windows (a stable, well-identified number, unlike the BABIP and HR auxes) and the EYE curve
takes the channel's out-of-frame slope to zero in every panel.

LEVEL move — identical composite SLOPES to five decimals in all four panels, ΔwPearson identical

  IN FRAME   fit 2042+2043 → measure 2042+2043  -0.00049 → -0.00060   (Δ -0.00011)

i.e. about 1e-4 wOBA per SD — real, correctly signed, and an order below the composite's own
noise. THE DECISION: DO NOT correct it, on this evidence. (i) The LEVEL half is anchor-absorbable
— every per-role anchor removes a constant — and the harness target credits the same fixed 6 on
the OBSERVED side, so the correction is invisible to every metric we rank on by construction.
(ii) The SLOPE half is what an anchor cannot absorb, and closing it moves the composite by less
than the composite's own interval. (iii) Adoption is not free: RawHitting carries no HBP field,
so a fitted HBP reaches BIP but NOT the wOBA numerator downstream (flagged in raw-poly.ts) — a
scoring-core change for a benefit measured at zero. If the HBP constant is ever revisited, do it
as a LEVEL correction on its own merits (calibration honesty), not as part of an EYE-axis fix.

WHAT THIS ARTIFACT SETTLES. The EYE-axis residual family is CLOSED on league data: one leg was
tried and failed; the intermediates were tried and failed; all four together were tried and
failed — and the reason is now structural rather than statistical. The composite is a SIX-channel
cancellation, so a four-leg fix cannot zero it; and the two legs with enough leverage to move the
deliverable (BABIP, HR) are the two the data cannot pin down at ~75 cards per window. The
residual finding stands as a true description of where the form misses; it is not a mandate to
add terms.
THE ONE THING THAT WOULD CHANGE THE ANSWER, and it is now a sharper ask than after the K-only
run: not a better metric — MORE CARDS PER WINDOW. The HR leg is the first term in this family
with real leverage (CI-clear ±0.005 on wPearson); what disqualifies it is that its estimate swings
-0.22/-0.35 between adjacent windows. If a future retrain widens the window (or the tournament
corpus supplies cards at this PA bar), re-run THIS ladder first — the machinery is in place and
the question becomes decidable rather than under-determined. The candidates stay in FORM_ENTRIES
so every future scoreboard re-measures them; nothing is deployed and no default changed.

WHAT THIS ARTIFACT SETTLES. The EYE-axis residual family is CLOSED on league data: one leg was

cancellation, so a four-leg fix cannot zero it; and the two legs with enough leverage to move the
deliverable (BABIP, HR) are the two the data cannot pin down at ~75 cards per window. The
residual finding stands as a true description of where the form misses; it is not a mandate to
