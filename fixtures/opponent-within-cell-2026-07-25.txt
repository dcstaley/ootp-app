################################################################################
## 3. LEAVE-ONE-CELL-OUT — the transfer gate. Fit on three platoon cells, predict the fourth.
################################################################################

  Design points are (league, season, cell) exposure-weighted cell means. The BASELINE is season
  fixed effects with the slope pinned to ZERO — i.e. "the held-out cell behaves like the average of
  the other three, in that season". Two slope estimators are tried against it:
     A  POOLED on the three training cells (season FE + slope) — what a naive fit would carry.
     B  WITHIN-(cell×season) on the three training cells — the pure opponent-quality response.
  SKILL = 1 − MSE(model)/MSE(baseline), exposure-weighted over all four held-out cells. Positive
  skill = the opposing-quality term genuinely transfers to a platoon cell it never saw. A platoon
  dummy cannot produce positive skill here; a real quality response should.

  A third reading, LEVEL-FREE (skill W), is added at audit: inside the held-out cell, demean x and y
  by season and ask whether the training slope predicts the ACROSS-LEAGUE spread. Skill B asks the
  slope to explain the held-out cell's platoon LEVEL as well; skill W asks only that the quality
  response itself carry over. A channel can fail B for a level reason and still pass W.

################################################################################
## 4. NOTES, CONTRADICTIONS, AND WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
################################################################################

  VERTEX-PIN PARITY (verified in code, reported not fixed). The deployed trainer
  (server.saveTrainedModel) fits fitHitForm(RAWPOLY_HIT, …, vertexPinned) +
  fitPitForm(PARETO_PIT, …, vertexPinned). The bake-off harness's hitFormModel/pitFormModel
  (src/training/forms.ts ~620-625) call fitHitForm(form, train) / fitPitForm(form, train) with NO
  pins argument, so every FORM_ENTRIES candidate is fitted UNPINNED. The active artifact records
  vertexPinned = [{"channel":"pit.hr","pinZ":2.652129826822849}].
  ⇒ the harness has never scored what ships, and PARETO_PIT is not in FORM_ENTRIES at all.
  This measurement does not use the harness fit path — every number above is read off the DEPLOYED
  artifact's own eventForm — so the discrepancy does not touch these results. It is recorded here
  because any later comparison built on FORM_ENTRIES would silently be off-deployment.

  WHAT THE SURVEY GOT RIGHT AND WHAT IT DID NOT. The survey's shares are reproduced at its own
  grain (section 1, SURVEY column) — they are arithmetically correct. What they do not establish is
  a response to opponent QUALITY, because the regressor was a four-level platoon dummy. Section 2
  is the missing test; section 3 is the missing gate.

  WHAT REMAINS TRUE REGARDLESS. The model has no opponent term, and every event is a joint
  pitcher-batter outcome — that is a structural fact, not a measurement. What this run bounds is
  how much of the MEASURED residual structure is attributable to opposing quality as opposed to
  platoon structure the model already fails to carry for a different reason.

  AUDIT OF THIS TOOL (it was written but never run; it was checked before it was trusted). Verified:
  row construction, composition, residual definitions, weights and the complementary-pricing slopes
  are line-for-line the survey's, so SURVEY-column numbers are a true replication, not a re-derivation.
  Three gaps were found and closed: (1) the pre-committed CARD-cluster bootstrap is the LENIENT
  cluster here, because opposing quality is constant inside a league-season while the same cards recur
  in every league — a league-season cluster CI was added (§2B); (2) no CARD fixed effect was tested,
  the strongest design this corpus allows — added (§2B); (3) the whole-opposing-profile question was
  not implemented at all — added out-of-sample (§2C). The level-free LOCO reading (skill W, §3) was
  added so a transfer failure caused by the platoon LEVEL is not read as a failure of the response.

  LIMITS. (a) Leagues are not randomised: a league's opposing quality correlates with its own card
  population, so a within-cell slope is still observational. (b) The same cards recur across
  league-seasons, which is why every CI is a card-cluster bootstrap and why distinct-card counts
  sit beside every row count. (c) Opposition is proxied by the QUALIFYING population of the
  league-season, not by the actual per-plate-appearance opponent — the same approximation the survey
  made, kept deliberately so the two are comparable.
  (d) Same-side exposure is manager-selected, so the platoon contrast is a property of this corpus.
