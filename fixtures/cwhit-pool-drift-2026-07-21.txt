== WHY THE OLD FIXTURES CANNOT SERVE AS THE DEPTH CONTROL ==
  NEW window is 2026-07-05..07-19 (15d) for all ten cells. OLD windows differ PER TIER:
    iron/bronze  2026-06-28..07-12 (15d)  -> 8 days overlap
    silver       2026-06-28..07-11 (14d)  -> 7 days
    diamond      2026-06-28..07-11 (14d)  -> 7 days
    gold         2026-06-28..07-05 ( 8d)  -> ONE DAY of overlap
  Old gold also covered only 59 of 506 tournaments, old silver 95 of 774.
  ⇒ an old-vs-full comparison confounds depth with the window. The derived top-100 of the
    new corpus is the correct depth control, which is what the A/B used.

== CORRECTION TO AN EARLIER CLAIM OF MINE ==
  I previously wrote that the old fixtures are rounded while the new carry full float. That is
  true only for wOBA/AVG/OBP/SLG/wOBAA. The RATE columns used above carry the SAME display
  precision in both captures (2dp K9/BB9/HR9, 3dp BABIP, 1dp BB%/SO%/HR600), so the rate
  deltas are symmetric — but QUANTISED, which is why they are reported against the quantum.
