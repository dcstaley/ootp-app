  HOW TO READ THE PART-7 SIGN FLIPS. residuals.ts reports valuation error as (actual - pred) x
  1000 for pitchers, i.e. POSITIVE = the model OVER-VALUES the card, which is -1000 x the DwOBAA

  Stuff" (weakly agreeing with the audit) and NEW stu linear +0.07 reads as "over-valuation grows
  very slightly with Stuff" (weakly disagreeing). BOTH ARE NEGLIGIBLE: 0.07 points per SD against
  a per-card error scale of 10.6 and interaction terms above 1.0. The flip is a real change of
  sign on a quantity indistinguishable from zero, and nothing in the verdict rests on it. The
  hrr changes are the larger ones (linear -0.23 -> -0.50, quad +0.11 -> -0.32) and they point the
  SAME way as the out-of-frame conditional HR-rate coefficient (+0.000820/SD on the DwOBAA sign
  convention), so the corrected instrument is more internally consistent than the old one, not

################################################################################
### VERDICT
################################################################################

  TWO SEPARATE ANSWERS, and they must not be blurred: WHICH CHANNEL carries the bias (Parts 1–3,
  the original question) and WHICH RATING AXES drive it (Parts 4–8, the scope amendment). The
  channel answer is strong and unchanged; the axis answer amends the brief's Stuff-only framing.

  ── PART A: WHICH CHANNEL ──

  THE CHANNEL IS BABIP — the pitcher CONTACT-RATE channel (the pit.h curve). Of the six channels that
  exhaustively decompose the wOBA-allowed bias, BABIP carries +0.000032 wOBA per Stuff
  point — 5.4× the next-largest channel (K, -0.000006) and +137% of the composite
  slope of +0.000023. Every other channel is at or below the +0.000006 level and they
  partially cancel one another. The carrier is the CONTACT RATE, not HR, not BB, not K.

  STRENGTH OF THE CLAIM, STATED HONESTLY. Pooled, the BABIP slope's bootstrap CI is
  [-0.000022, +0.000072] — it does NOT quite exclude zero at 95%, and so does the composite
  it explains (+0.000023 [-0.000070, +0.000094]). N=149 rows over only 67 CARDS — and the CIs are
  CARD-CLUSTERED, so they are the honest width — is a small instrument. So this
  is a LOCATION result, not a fresh significance result: the audit already established (out of
  frame, larger sample) THAT the Stuff-correlated bias exists and is CI-clear; this measurement
  says WHICH channel it flows through, and the answer is unambiguous by margin (5.4×) even where
  the individual CI is not. The vL composite IS CI-clear here (+0.000075 [+0.000015, +0.000144]),
  and BABIP is its largest channel (+0.000041, +55%) — the same verdict on the one sub-sample
  where the composite clears on its own.

  SHAPE: a LEVEL/quintile pattern (neither linear nor cubic partial is CI-clear on its own).
  The pooled quintile pattern runs -1.451 (lowest-Stuff quintile) → +0.879 pts×1000
  (highest), i.e. the model over-states the contact
  the high-Stuff pitcher allows and under-states it at the bottom — a LEVEL SHIFT ACROSS THE STUFF
  RANGE rather than a tail blow-up, with no CI-clear curvature. Read this as a CHANNEL shape only —
  whether the driving AXIS is really Stuff is settled below (scope amendment), not here.

  HAND ASYMMETRY: on the carrier channel, vR +0.000012 vs vL +0.000041 — each point estimate
  sits inside the other's CI, so the CHANNEL slope is NOT distinguishably hand-split.
  BUT THE COMPOSITE IS: vR -0.000040 vs vL +0.000075 — opposite signs, and only vL
  clears its CI. The Stuff-correlated under-credit is concentrated on the vs-LHB side in frame.
  That is a real hand-split flag on the COMPOSITE even though the carrier channel's own slope is
  not separable by hand at this N — carry it forward as an open dimension, not a settled one.

  WHERE K SITS (closing the pre-step): K contributes -0.000006 per Stuff point,
  NEGATIVE — it pushes the composite bias DOWN as Stuff rises, exactly the sign the pre-step
  predicted. K is not the mechanism; it is a PARTIAL OFFSET to it, and it enters wOBAA only
  indirectly (a strikeout has no wOBA weight — it moves wOBAA solely by removing a ball in play).
  HR contributes -0.000000 and BB +0.000004;
  HR is an order below the carrier ⇒ the HR candidate named in the brief is NOT the seat of the
  under-credit. Of the two contact-side candidates (pit.pbabip vs pit.hrr), it is pbabip.

  READ THE CONSTRUCTION CAVEAT WITH THIS — it is what makes the result both expected and useful.
  pit.bb, pit.k and pit.hr all carry Stuff in their fitted design under the DEPLOYED PARETO form
  (pit.k's own driver IS Stuff, as a RAW QUADRATIC — so its residual is orthogonal to z_stu AND
  z_stu²; pit.bb and pit.hr carry the stuffAug ln(Stuff) linear leg on top of log-Control and
  raw-quad-HRR respectively), so their in-frame Stuff slopes are pushed toward
  zero BY LEAST-SQUARES. pit.h does not — Stuff never enters the contact-rate design at all. So this
  measurement does NOT prove BB/HR are innocent OUT of frame. What it does establish is sharper and
  sufficient: (a) the deployed pitcher form has NO STUFF PATHWAY INTO THE CONTACT RATE, a structural
  hole rather than a mis-shaped curve; (b) the Stuff-shaped contact error that such a pathway would
  have absorbed measures +0.000032/Stuff-point in frame, the same order as the audit's
  out-of-frame composite (+0.00007) and the dominant term of the in-frame one; and (c) the K route,
  the only Stuff pathway the form does have, moves the composite the WRONG WAY.

  ── PART B: WHICH RATING AXES — SINGLE-AXIS OR SHAPE-DEPENDENT? (the scope amendment) ──

  The defect is best described as MULTI-AXIS AND ADDITIVE — genuinely rating-SHAPE dependent, but with NO interaction corner.
  That is a THIRD answer, distinct from both options the amendment posed, and it is the one the
  data supports: 2 of the 4 pitcher ratings carry an INDEPENDENT, CI-clear slope on the bias
  (Stuff +0.001227/SD, Control -0.001722/SD), each surviving
  when the others are held fixed — so no single rating tells you the error, exactly as the doctrine
  says. But the 2-way interaction terms are NOT distinguishable from noise, so the bias is not
  concentrated in a shape CORNER either: it is ADDITIVE across several axes.

  The numbers behind that call:

   1. AXIS RANKING (in frame). By cubic R² on the composite residual:
      Control 0.034  >  Stuff 0.032  >  HR-rate 0.013  >  pBABIP 0.006.
      Stuff ranks 2 of 4. Control carries MORE residual structure than Stuff, and the
      conditional coefficient set puts Control (-0.000999/SD) ahead of Stuff (+0.000092/SD).
      The brief's framing on Stuff alone was NOT the best axis.
      (All four axis CIs cover zero in frame — this ranking is by point estimate, and at N=149 it
      is an ordering of weak signals. It is corroborating evidence, not the call.)
   2. CONDITIONING, IN FRAME. Marginal Stuff +0.000472/SD → conditional +0.000092/SD
      (20% retained). Both CIs cover zero, so this is directional only.
   3. CONDITIONING, OUT OF FRAME — THE DECISIVE ONE. On the cwhit sample (N=546) where the
      marginal effect IS established: marginal +0.001753/SD (CI-clear) → conditional
      +0.001227/SD [+0.000095, +0.002366] (still CI-clear) — 70% retained.
      The Stuff slope SURVIVES holding the other three ratings fixed ⇒ Stuff is a GENUINE
      INDEPENDENT AXIS on the sample that matters, not a proxy for Control.
      Out-of-frame stu×con interaction: +0.000152/SD² [-0.000642, +0.000989] covers 0.
   4. SHAPE CORNERS, TESTED AGAINST A PERMUTATION NULL. Largest 3×3 interaction cell:
      Stuff×Control +0.00590 (card-block p=0.462, row-shuffle p=0.473),
      Stuff×HR-rate +0.00565 (card-block p=0.355, row-shuffle p=0.368),
      against a Stuff main-effect quintile spread of only +0.00093. NEITHER corner beats its noise
      null — the cells hold 11–23 rows and corners that large arise by chance. The eye-catching
      corner is NOT evidence; this is exactly the trap the amendment warns about, and it does not
      fire here.
   5. INDEPENDENT INSTRUMENT (Part 7, the old record's own): meta-model systematic r² 0.083;
      largest interaction stu×hrr +1.05 EXCEEDS the largest linear hrr -0.50,
      and the stu LINEAR term is only +0.07 with a quad of -0.24. On the old
      record's OWN instrument the structure looks interaction-led rather than Stuff-linear-led.
      TENSION WITH ITEM 4, FLAGGED NOT HIDDEN: that meta-model's interaction coefficients are
      ridge point estimates with NO significance test, while the corners that WERE tested
      (permutation null, item 4; bootstrap CI, item 3) did not fire. Where they disagree, trust
      the tested reads — item 5 is suggestive only, and it is why the call above is ADDITIVE
      multi-axis rather than shape-corner.

  CONTRADICTION WITH THE OLD RECORD, STATED PLAINLY. The archived investigation recorded "per-BIP
  hit rate has ZERO Stuff signal (confirms engine: no harder-contact)" and used that to rule the
  contact channel out. Part 2 ranks the CONTACT-RATE channel FIRST (+0.000032/Stuff-point,
  5.4× the next channel). These disagree. Both can be partly right and the difference is
  informative: the old test asked whether the RAW per-BIP hit rate correlates with Stuff (an engine
  question — does high Stuff produce weaker contact); this one asks whether the MODEL'S BABIP
  RESIDUAL correlates with Stuff (a model question — does the fitted contact curve mis-place cards
  by Stuff). A curve fit on pBABIP alone can leave a Stuff-shaped residual even when the raw rate
  carries no Stuff signal, because the OTHER channels' errors move BIP and the pBABIP→hits curve
  is evaluated at the wrong BIP. Note also the old test predates the deployed 42-43 fit and the
  stuffAug legs. THE OLD "ZERO STUFF SIGNAL IN CONTACT" CONCLUSION SHOULD NOT BE CARRIED FORWARD
  UNEXAMINED — re-running it against the deployed artifact is the cheap way to settle it, and it
  is NOT settled here.

  WHAT THE AMENDMENT CHANGES ABOUT THE REMEDY. The pre-amendment draft of this artifact named a
  linear Stuff aux term on pit.h (the analogue of the stuffAug legs pit.bb and pit.hr carry).
  THE SHAPE READS AMEND IT — the CHANNEL stands, the AXIS SET does not. A pit.h aux keyed on Stuff
  ALONE would leave the other CI-clear axes uncorrected (notably Control at -0.001722/SD,
  the opposite sign to Stuff — the classic low-Stuff/high-Control over-valuation).
  The remedy shape is therefore a MULTI-RATING aux on pit.h — ADDITIVE (no interaction term is
  warranted: every corner test came back inside its null) — with the axis set taken from the
  CONDITIONAL coefficients above, not from the marginal Stuff slope. Which ratings and what
  magnitudes requires a fit, and fitting is out of scope here.
  NOT a K-curve change (ruled out by the pre-step — wrong sign). NOT an HR-curve change (an order
  too small here). NOT an anchor change (the anchor provably cannot absorb a rating-correlated
  bias — that is the audit's whole point).
  PROPOSED, NOT FIT, NOT WIRED. The form ruling is Fable's.
