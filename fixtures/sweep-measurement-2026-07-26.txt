═══ 0. MULTIPLICITY — READ THIS BEFORE ANY COUNT BELOW ═══════════════════════════════════════════

  This run tests 126 LEVEL cells and 126 SPREAD (free-slope) cells that carry a verdict
  — 252 verdict-bearing tests in total, plus 0 THIN and 0 DEAD cells that carry none, plus
  the per-hand contrasts and heterogeneity tests reported in §5 and §7.

  AT α = 0.05, 12.6 OF THOSE 252 CELLS ARE EXPECTED TO BE "CI-CLEAR" WITH NOTHING REAL BEHIND THEM.
  A raw count of significant cells is therefore NOT a finding, and this run never reports one as
  such. What can be read: (a) the SHAPE of the counts — whether CI-clear cells concentrate in a
  channel/stratum far beyond 12.6; (b) individual EFFECT SIZES against their own MDEs; (c) the
  three pre-registered priors in §8, which were written before any number was computed and are
  graded PRIOR HELD / PRIOR VIOLATED one by one.

  These tests are NOT independent (channels share a BIP chain: any change to BB/K/HR changes BIP
  and therefore hits — SYSTEM_MAP §1), so 12.6 is an ORDER OF MAGNITUDE for the chance count, not
  an exact expectation. Positive dependence makes the true false-positive count MORE variable,
  not smaller. No Bonferroni/FDR adjustment is applied: this is an EXPLORATORY sweep whose job is
  to locate structure for a later, pre-registered test — adjusting here would trade a false-
  positive problem for a false-negative one without earning a confirmatory claim either way.

═══ 2. GRAIN — stated for EVERY observed-side statistic, as FORMAT_FACTS requires ════════════════

  THE CORPUS HAS NO ROW/APPEARANCE GRAIN AT ALL. cwhit's observed tables are one line per
  (CID, VLvl), pooled by him over every instance of the format inside the capture window. There is
  no per-game and no per-instance record anywhere in it. So every observed statistic in this run is
  a CARD-GRAIN aggregate, and our predicted line is the same grain by construction (one line per
  (card, VLvl)). No statistic below is a ROW-grain (per-appearance) statistic. Where the word
  "row" appears it means an OBSERVED TABLE ROW = (CID, VLvl), i.e. a card at a variant level.

  THE ONE GRAIN HAZARD, AND THE CORRECTION APPLIED:  (CID, VLvl) is NOT the card. A card and its
  v5 variant are TWO observed rows describing ONE underlying card with ONE rating shape, so their
  errors are strongly dependent. Every LEVEL differential here is therefore computed at CARD grain
  with a CLUSTER-ROBUST SE, clusters = Card ID; every bootstrap resamples CLUSTERS; every
  permutation permutes labels at CLUSTER level. The design effect this buys is MEASURED per
  statistic in §3 — never assumed.

  TWO AGGREGATIONS ARE PRINTED SIDE BY SIDE and they are different questions:
    CARD    = unweighted mean over cards. THE PRIMARY. It is the convention the shipped ramps'
              needs are estimated under (unweighted free slope), so it is the comparable number.
    USAGE   = BF/PA-weighted mean. What the format's aggregate line actually looked like. It tilts
              toward the most-used cards, and winners play more games (memory: tournament
              survivorship), so it is a DEPLOYMENT-weighted read, never a talent read.

  DERIVED OBSERVED QUANTITIES (flagged wherever they appear):
    · hitter K% — cwhit publishes K/AB; converted to K/PA in the shared builder.
    · hitter GAP/600 — reconstructed from XBHpct + AVG/OBP/BBpct. NOT a published column.
    · pitcher GAP — NOT MEASURABLE AT ALL (see §4). Not faked, not estimated, not counted.

  READ: the LEVEL design effect is the load-bearing one — it multiplies the width of every level

  TWO READINGS OF ONE QUESTION, and they are not interchangeable:
    dcv   = SD(pred) / SD_deconv(obs). Needs the binomial noise model. UNREL when the 97.5th-pct
            bootstrap noise share reaches 1.0 (sampling variance may exceed TOTAL observed
            variance) — then the deconvolved SD collapses and dcv is not a measurement.
    slope = free slope of obs~pred, THE ESTIMAND THE SHIPPED RAMPS ARE FITTED UNDER (amendment-2,
            ruling (z), tools/fit-kspread-c3.ts). NOISE-IMMUNE: observed sampling noise lands
            entirely in the residual. slope = 1 ⇔ calibrated spacing; slope > 1 ⇔ we UNDER-react
            by that factor and the channel needs amplifying; slope < 1 ⇔ we over-react.

  not on their answers. A per-channel LEVEL bias that is uniform within a pool is a CONVENTION at

  asking "do the fourteen formats carry different conventions". At these N the level SEs are tiny

  almost every channel — it cannot DISCRIMINATE between channels, which is the only thing the
  priors ask of it. The FREE SLOPE is what the shipped corrections are estimated on, is what every
  standing spread verdict is stated in, and is NOT absorbable by a mean-pinning anchor. The level Q
  is still printed, so the demotion is visible rather than a quiet omission.
