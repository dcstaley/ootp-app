# READING — descriptive
################################################################################

THE ELIGIBLE-POOL ASSUMPTION IS OFF BY 0.18 TO 1.36 SD. That is the headline. The
coordinate the correction is computed on describes a field that, in several cells, sits
more than a full pool-SD away from the one actually faced.

THE ERROR GROWS AS THE WINDOW WIDENS, because coverage collapses: 38.8% of iron's eligible
hitters ever play, against 6.4% of diamond's. A wider window admits far more cards than it
causes to be used, so "eligible" and "faced" diverge fastest exactly where the window is
widest. Usage is also heavily concentrated — the top 10% of cards take 44-69% of all PA.

THE SIGN IS EXPECTED; THE MAGNITUDE AND THE ORDERING ARE THE FINDING. Cards that get played
are better than the average eligible card, so a positive divergence everywhere is not
surprising and is not by itself evidence of anything. What is informative is (i) how large
it is, and (ii) that it is NOT monotone in tier on four of five channels.

GOLD BREAKS THE ORDER ON TWO CHANNELS AND PEAKS ON A THIRD: BABIP 1.238 (below silver's
1.336) and Gap 0.793 (below silver's 1.035), while Eye peaks at gold 0.771, above diamond's
0.411. So gold's realized opposition is not simply "further off" — it is off in a different
pattern from its neighbours.

RELATION TO ITEM 5, stated as a juxtaposition and NOT as a causal claim: item 5 found gold's
high-K pitchers are used about half as much as its own mid-K group, inverting the pattern of
every other tier. Item 4 finds gold's realized opposition diverges most on Eye and breaks the
tier order on BABIP and Gap. Both are statements that gold's REALIZED play differs in shape
from what its eligible pool implies. Neither shows that this produces the 1.78 K need, and
nothing here tests that link.

CAVEATS THAT LIMIT THIS
 · VLvl-5 VARIANT ROWS ARE EXCLUDED ENTIRELY (104 observed rows at gold, 52-170 per tier).
   Variants are generated at runtime and are not in the catalog CSV, so their PA is absent
   from the realized weighting. The realized field is therefore measured on base cards only,
   and variants are genuinely played. This is the largest single limitation here.
 · The observed tables carry a site-side minPA=30 floor, so "never played" conflates truly
   unused with used-below-30-PA, and the concentration figures sit on a left-truncated
   distribution.
 · Eligibility is Card Value <= cap only; no positional or roster-feasibility filter.
 · The eligible POOLS are nested. (Note the contrast with item 5: the well-sampled OBSERVED
   sets are nearly disjoint, 1-3% shared. Pool-derived statistics like these ARE nested;
   observed per-tier statistics are not.)
