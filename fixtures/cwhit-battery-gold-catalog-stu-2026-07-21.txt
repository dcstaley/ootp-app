VERDICT: the sign flip is ABSENT. The mechanism is NOT confirmed by the catalog.

1. THE HEADLINE (corr between predicted value and Stuff vR).
   FULL POOL:   iron +0.349 | bronze +0.276 | silver +0.337 | gold +0.383 | diamond +0.449
   Positive at every tier, gold included, and gold sits exactly where a smooth trend puts it —
   between silver (+0.337) and diamond (+0.449). Excluding bronze the series is monotone
   increasing; gold is not a break in it, it is a point on it. There is no sign flip and no
   gold-specific weakening. The hypothesis predicted near-zero-or-negative at gold and positive
   elsewhere; the data show positive at gold and gold is the SECOND STRONGEST of the five.

   WITHIN TOP-100: iron -0.033 | bronze -0.214 | silver +0.065 | gold -0.039 | diamond -0.025
   Inside the model-selected top the stuff correlation collapses to ~0 EVERYWHERE — the expected
   consequence of conditioning on a near-frontier (once you hold predicted value nearly fixed,
   the channels trade off against each other, so within-cohort correlations shrink toward 0 and
   can go slightly negative). Gold's -0.039 is indistinguishable from iron's -0.033 and diamond's
   -0.025. The one tier that does stand out on this view is BRONZE at -0.214, i.e. the opposite
   tier from the one the hypothesis named. Gold is unremarkable on both views.

2. IS GOLD'S TOP STUFF-POOR? NO — it is stuff-ABOVE-pool.
   Top-25 stuff profile, in pool-SD units: iron +0.29 | bronze -0.23 | silver +0.50 |
   gold +0.41 | diamond +0.98. Gold's top 25 averages Stuff vR 86.0 against a pool mean of 77.8.
   The only tier whose top 25 is stuff-BELOW its pool is bronze (-0.23sd). "Low-stuff best gold
   pitchers" is not what the catalog contains.

3. IS GOLD'S TOP SECONDARY-HEAVY? YES — but so is every tier's, and gold is not the extreme.
   Top-25 profile in pool SDs, gold: stu +0.41, con +1.01, pHR +2.18, pBABIP +0.90. The secondary
   channels do dominate the selection — pHR is the single strongest lever at every tier (+1.51 to
   +2.28) and the full-pool corr(value, pHR) runs +0.465..+0.661, always above corr(value, stu).
   But that is a MODEL-WIDE property, not a gold property: diamond's profile (+0.98/+1.04/+2.07/
   +1.04) is secondary-heavy in the same shape and more extreme on three of four channels, and
   bronze is more pHR-tilted than gold (+2.28). Gold is secondary-heavy in absolute terms and
   ORDINARY in relative terms. "Gold's top is secondary-heavy" is true; "gold's top is secondary-
   heavy IN A WAY OTHER TIERS ARE NOT" is false.

4. WHAT IS ACTUALLY MONOTONE. Every full-pool correlation rises with tier on all four channels
   (stu +0.276..+0.449 ex-iron, con +0.159..+0.368, pHR +0.465..+0.661, pBABIP +0.091..+0.318),
   and pool means and SDs rise with tier on all four. Richer pools give the model more room and
   every channel's grip on value strengthens together. Gold is an extremum on ZERO of the eight
   correlation series and on zero of the four top-25 profiles.

5. PARTIAL/NEGATIVE ELEMENTS, stated so nothing is rescued. Iron breaks the monotone full-pool
   stu series (+0.349, above bronze and silver) and bronze is the within-top-100 outlier
   (-0.214) and the only negative top-25 stuff profile (-0.23sd). If a tier's pool has an odd
   stuff axis, the catalog says it is BRONZE, not gold. That is a different claim from the one
   pre-registered here and this tool was not designed to test it.

CAVEATS THAT BOUND EVERY NUMBER ABOVE
 · The five pools are NESTED by card value (iron subset of bronze subset of silver subset of
   gold subset of diamond). Gold's 1420-pitcher pool literally contains silver's 1230. Cross-tier
   contrasts are therefore NOT independent samples and the apparent smoothness of the tier series
   is partly mechanical — consecutive tiers share most of their cards. A gold-specific break would
   have had to be large to show through that sharing; none of any size is present.
 · RATING SPACE / CATALOG ONLY. Predicted value here is the model's raw predicted allowed wOBA on
   untransformed catalog ratings — the deployed field-SELECTION basis, not a played outcome. This
   says nothing about what actually gets used, at what rate, or with what result. It tests only
   whether the claimed mechanism is present in the catalog the model reads, which is where the
   hypothesis located it.
 · Every number is labelled FULL POOL or TOP-K. Nothing in the READING treats a model-selected
   cohort's moments as the pool's, which was the 2026-07-21 retraction's error.

DESCRIPTIVE ONLY. Nothing fitted, nothing changed, no default moved, nothing proposed.
