### VERDICT

  REAL ASYMMETRY — power ruled out

  The power explanation fails at the first hurdle: vL is NOT the weaker split. It carries 72 rows to vR's 77,
  78% of the BF, but 11.6 cubic-contrast ESS against vR's 9.6 — and a NARROWER bootstrap CI
  (1.229 vs 1.556). vL resolves the cubic at least as well as vR does, so "vL is flat" is not "vL is unresolved".
  Degrading vR to vL-equivalent BF, its cubic stays negative in 100% of draws and CI-clear in 86%,
  and only 1.1% of vL-powered measurements of a vR-sized effect would read as high as vL's +0.0537.
  CAVEAT, stated plainly: the direct vR−vL difference is -0.8682, CI [-1.9657, -0.0100], bootstrap p=0.049 —
  a BOUNDARY case: the CI edge sits on zero, so the CI-clear flag itself is not a stable summary.
  So: the vR-concentration is NOT a power artifact, but the two hands are only marginally distinguishable from
  each other. The hand LEVEL gap (+0.54/600) is a separate claim and is NOT resolved (see §2).

  Supporting numbers: vR cubic -0.8145 [-1.8521, -0.2957] (width 1.556) vs vL cubic
  +0.0537 [-0.6532, +0.5759] (width 1.229) — vL's CI is 0.79× vR's width.
  Information: vR 77 rows / 1,060,686 BF / cubic-ESS 9.6;  vL 72 rows / 832,512 BF /
  cubic-ESS 11.6  (vL carries 78% of vR's BF and 120% of its cubic-contrast ESS).
