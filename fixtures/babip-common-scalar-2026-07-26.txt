## 4. WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE FLAT SCALAR (stated so the null is not unfalsifiable)

  A flat scalar dies when the per-format CI intersection goes EMPTY on an estimand and bar where the
  cell driving it is NOT the thinnest one — i.e. when leave-one-out cannot restore it. On the gate
  estimand it survives comfortably at both bars. On the primary estimand it survives at BF >= 600
  with almost no slack and fails at BF >= 1000 on a single N=19 cell that LOO restores.

  BUT DO NOT TAKE THE LOO RESCUE AS AN ALL-CLEAR. The BF >= 1000 INTERSECTION is one cell; the
  BF >= 600 primary-estimand I2 is NOT (§2 LOO: 68–73% whichever format is dropped). Those are two
  different statistics and only the first is a thin-cell artefact. What survives depth-scepticism is
  'the intersection has not been shown empty', not 'the formats agree'.

  SO THE HONEST STATE IS: NOT FALSIFIED, AND NO LONGER COMFORTABLE. Both readings are one depth
  increment from being decidable — stratum A primary-estimand SEs run 0.08–0.31, and the
  question is settled by roughly a halving of them, i.e. ~4x the judged rows per format. That is the
  SAME depth requirement the coordinate run named in its §11(c), reached from a different direction,
  and it is exactly why this estimate is an INPUT to a post-pull fit rather than a fit.

  ONE MISREADING TO KILL BEFORE IT STARTS. bronzequick reads 1.503 on the primary estimand, which
  is close to BUILD-3's famous 1.48. THAT IS A COINCIDENCE OF TWO DIFFERENT ESTIMANDS, NOT A
  REPRODUCTION. BUILD-3's published per-tier BABIP slopes are mmse().slope — the UNWEIGHTED
  free-intercept slope (tools/fit-pitspread-hrbab.ts, `mBab.slope.est`), i.e. this run's GATE
  column, where bronze reads 1.325. The coordinate run's finding that 1.48 is not recoverable
  on any configuration reachable today stands, and nothing here revives it.

  (2) THE FORM IT MUST TAKE — and the hMul constraint DECIDES this, it is not a style choice.
      src/model/pool-transform.ts applyPitSpread leg 3 is the only place a pitcher-BABIP spread
      correction exists. It:
        · measures bab0 = nHH / BIP0 on the ORIGINAL BIP
          (BIP0 = 600 − BB − K − HR − BIP_ADJ, i.e. AFTER the K and HR legs have already moved BIP);
        · applies bab2 = clamp(BAB̄_pool + s·(bab0 − BAB̄_pool), 0, 0.6)  — a RATE-space pivot about

        · rides the RATE MULTIPLIER: e.hMul *= bab2/bab0. THIS IS THE BINDING CONSTRAINT.
          pitchingComponents re-derives nHH from the rating, so a COUNT-only change is DISCARDED by
          the BIP recompute (the RawHitting.hMul lesson). A hit-RATE correction that does not ride
          hMul is not a correction at all — it is a no-op with a plausible-looking fit behind it.
        · then rescales nHH/XBH mix-preserving on the NEW BIP.
      ⇒ THE FITTED OBJECT IS A RATE-SPACE SPREAD MULTIPLIER ABOUT BAB̄_pool, applied PRE-BIP and
        PRE-ERA. It is NOT a level shift (the anchor owns level, SYSTEM_MAP §2), NOT a count
        correction, and NOT a per-hand or per-role quantity.

      THE FUNCTIONAL WRAPPER: the C3 family at its constant limit, NOT a bare number.
          s(g) = 1 + A·(g/G0)^q,  G0 = 20,  q -> 0  ⇒  s(g) = 1 + A for every g > 0, s(g<=0) = 1 EXACTLY.
      Two reasons this wrapper and not a literal constant: (a) s(g<=0) = 1 is the one principled part
