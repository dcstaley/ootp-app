§3 — IS 50 STILL RIGHT? (src/server/server.ts:235 cites tools/field-size.ts; the citation now lives
     on FIELD_N in src/scoring-core/pool-stats.ts)

  WHAT THE TOOL IS, read in full: a 32-line diagnostic whose own first line calls it THROWAWAY. It
  loads the TRAINING-league window 2039-2040 (all five leagues, base cards only: 239 vL / 239 vR
  observations), sorts each of the four categories by realized usage, and prints the PA/BF of the
  Nth-ranked card at ranks 25/50/75/100.

  RE-RUNNABLE: YES, unmodified — "League Files" exists with Model 2039 and Model 2040, and the run
  above reproduces (deterministic; same data, same hard-coded window).

  WHAT IT ACTUALLY VALIDATED: that in a real league, usage collapses somewhere between rank 50 and
  rank 100. At rank 50 the Nth card still has 2005 / 2394 PA and 1297 / 2718 BF; by rank 75 the
  pitcher side is down to 167 BF vL, and by rank 100 it is 10 BF vL / 20 BF vR — i.e. cards 75-100
  are barely played. That supports "about 50 cards constitute a realistic FIELD" in the sense of
  DEPLOYMENT, and it is a reasonable thing to have concluded.

  WHY IT DOES NOT VALIDATE FIELD_N FOR THE JOB FIELD_N DOES — three gaps, stated plainly:
   1. POPULATION. It ranks 239 league OBSERVATIONS. FIELD_N cuts a cohort out of the ELIGIBLE
      CATALOG (1200-3498 cards here). Different population, an order of magnitude apart in size.
   2. SELECTION RULE. It ranks by REALIZED USAGE (what people played). FIELD_N ranks by the MODEL's
      PREDICTED wOBA. The whole subject of this audit is that the second rule is endogenous; the
      first is not, and a validation of one is not a validation of the other. Note §2(a): even
      among the same catalog cards, swapping the ranking rule replaces 24-50% of the cohort.
   3. CURRENCY. WINDOW is hard-coded [2039, 2040] while the deployed model is league-42-43. The
      standing fact that the league frame strengthens every window means a size validated in one
      window is not validated forever. Testing 42-43 would require EDITING WINDOW — which would make
      it a different check — so it was not done, per the brief.

  VERDICT: FIELD_N = 50 IS NOT CURRENTLY VALIDATED FOR ITS ACTUAL JOB. Nothing here shows 50 is
  WRONG, and nothing here nominates a replacement — that is the arbitration's job, not this
  measurement's. What is now established is that the citation is load-bearing and cannot bear the
  load: it validates a deployment-depth claim about league usage, two model-windows ago, while the
  constant it justifies picks a model-ranked catalog cohort whose size demonstrably sets 53-110% of
  the pitcher K correction. Any rule the arbitration lands should be validated on the population and
  the selection rule that FIELD_N actually uses.

  ALSO NOTE, and do NOT collapse them: ANCHOR_N (calibrate.ts) shares the value 50 for a different
  purpose. It could not be swept here — calibrate() takes no cohort-size parameter, so varying it
  would have meant editing a production constant. Every calibrate row above therefore holds the
  anchor cohort at 50 and moves only the FIELD cohort. Given §1's finding that the anchor outputs
  are flat in FIELD_N, the coincidence is at least harmless today; it is still two constants.

INCIDENTAL FINDING (not asked for; flagged, not fixed) — THE COHORT IS NOT ENVIRONMENT-FREE.
  diamond-quick and diamond-cap-daily draw on the IDENTICAL 3498-card pool (both VAL<=99, no extra
  rules) and differ only in era/park. Their PITCHER cohorts are identical, but their HITTER cohorts
  are not (kRat mu 114.33 vs 114.83 at N=50), which moves gap.pit.stu 10.38 -> 9.88 and s_K
  1.335 -> 1.319. Mechanism: hitter selection ranks on assembleRawHittingWoba, which carries the
  era-scaled BASERUNNING term; the pitcher assembly has no such term. Since every pitcher-channel gap
  is crossed off the HITTER cohort, the pitcher K ramp inherits an era sensitivity through
  baserunning. This matters because production's reference ("league") field is CACHED on
  (activeModelId, catalogSource) only (server.ts referenceFieldStats), i.e. frozen across
  environments, while the POOL field is recomputed per tournament — so the two ends of the transform
  do not treat the environment the same way. Small in magnitude here; recorded for the static half.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY. No number here is a recommendation. Nothing was refitted, no ramp was
re-derived at another N, and the shipped constants are untouched. The one thing that changed is that
"50" is now a measured choice rather than an inherited one.
