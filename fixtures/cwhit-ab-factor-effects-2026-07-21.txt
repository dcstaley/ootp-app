== READ THIS FIRST: TWO FACTS THAT GOVERN EVERY DELTA BELOW ==

1. DEPTH WAS STRUCTURALLY NON-BINDING ON 25 OF 50 CELLS. For silver-pit, gold and diamond
   the Section-B N is byte-identical A->B, because the top-100 cut never bound there — those
   tiers had fewer than 100 cards above the 1000 bar to begin with. Their Δdepth = 0 is a
   STRUCTURAL zero, not "measured and found unchanged". Depth genuinely binds on 25 cells
   (iron pit/hit, bronze pit/hit, silver hit). The bar moves N in ALL 50.
   ⇒ Any average over all 50 cells UNDERSTATES depth by construction. Both views are given.

2. SECTION-A Δdepth IS CONFOUNDED; use Section B for the clean depth read. In A the
   projection join is 411 of 991 rows, so Section A is a range-restricted subsample; in B/C
   it is 2907 of 2907, so Section A IS Section B. The Section-A A->B delta therefore mixes
   the depth change with the DISAPPEARANCE of the projection-join subsetting.
   Quantified: Section A vs Section B dcv agreement — snapshot A r=0.804, mean |diff| 0.099,
   max 0.420; snapshots B and C r=1.000, max |diff| 0.000 across 45-46 numeric cells.
   That is the Section-A double-selection dissolving, measured.

== THE BAR'S COST, WHICH THE HEADLINE N-GAIN HIDES ==
  ORDERING DEGRADES UNDER THE BAR IN EVERY ONE OF THE 10 CHANNELS — the signed mean Δcorr and
  Δrho are negative for all ten, no channel improves on average. Heaviest: hit/BABIP -0.112,
  pit/HR9 -0.092, hit/wOBA -0.073, pit/wOBAA -0.049. Largest single: diamond/hit/BABIP
  corr -0.289, diamond/pit/K9 dcv -0.400.
  This is the expected price of admitting less-sampled cards, but it IS a price: the bar buys
  N (+357) and pays in per-card ordering agreement. dcv by contrast moves both ways by channel
  (pit/K9 -0.094 and hit/wOBA -0.052 down; hit/HR600 +0.070, pit/wOBAA +0.035 up).
