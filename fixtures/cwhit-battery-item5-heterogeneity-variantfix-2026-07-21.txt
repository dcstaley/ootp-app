# READING — the headline finding is HALF ARTIFACT, and the half that remains is real
################################################################################

FIRST, THE JOIN. Row-grain N is now 142/156/121/91/36 — the SAME per-tier N both fit
tools report. The python's 147/94 came from its own key-collision handling; the
fingerprint join is the program's join and these are the program's numbers.

1. THE INVERTED USAGE FINDING IS ROUGHLY HALF A VARIANT-SPLIT ARTIFACT. Gold's mean IP
   by K9 tercile, low/mid/high:

     ROW grain  (base and v5 separate)   930.7 / 1154.3 /  554.9    high/mid = 0.48
     CARD grain (base and v5 merged)    1045.6 / 1351.7 /  963.0    high/mid = 0.71

   The row grain reproduces the original reading (918/1129/549). Merging a card's base
   and variant rows nearly halves the effect. THE MECHANISM IS VISIBLE IN THE DATA: at
   row grain gold's high-K tercile is 33.3% v5 rows against 16.7% in its low tercile, so
   the high-K end was precisely the end whose usage was split across two rows.

   IT DOES NOT VANISH. high/mid by tier at CARD grain: iron 0.98, bronze 0.93, silver
   2.24, GOLD 0.71, diamond 1.38. Gold is still the only tier where the high-K group is
   used materially less than its mid group — but "used ~half as much, inverting every
   other tier" was an overstatement of a real, smaller effect.

2. WHAT SURVIVES UNCHANGED AT BOTH GRAINS:
   · gold has the widest K9 spread — SD 1.62 (row) / 1.67 (card), against 0.92-1.54 and
     1.13-1.51 elsewhere
   · gold has the most dispersed LOW-K subgroup — tercile SD 1.49 / 1.53 vs 0.24-1.04
   · gold has the largest internal gap in the middle 80% — 0.550 / 0.604, about double
     the others at row grain (at card grain diamond's 0.540 is comparable, on N=32)
   · the observed sets are nearly DISJOINT across tiers — 1-5 shared cards per adjacent
     pair at both grains. The nesting caveat applies to catalog-derived pool statistics,
     not to these.

3. ONE CLAIM DOES NOT SURVIVE — the role-split control. At row grain gold's
   starter-minus-reliever K9 gap is -0.39, the smallest of the first four, which is what
   licensed "role split does not explain it". At CARD grain gold is -0.82, the LARGEST of
   the five (iron -0.97 aside), and no longer distinctive. That reading was grain-
   dependent and should not be quoted. The underlying conclusion is untouched — nothing
   here says the role split explains gold — but the supporting number does not hold.

4. VARIANTS ARE NOT A MARGINAL POPULATION. 54-81% of well-sampled CARDS have a v5 row,
   and v5 rows carry 16-29% of well-sampled IP. Any observed-side statistic at row grain
   is a statistic about card-variant-levels, not about cards, and the two differ.

ESTABLISHES NO CAUSE. Gold still differs in KIND — wider, gappier, more dispersed at the
low end, with its high-K end used less than its middle. Nothing here links that to the
1.78 K need.
