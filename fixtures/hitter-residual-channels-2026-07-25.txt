  THE BIGGEST BIAS IS A LEVEL AND IT DOES NOT MATTER: +94% of the +0.00111 composite level is
  the HBP channel's fixed 6/600 constant, which any per-role anchor absorbs. THE BIGGEST BIAS THAT
  DOES MATTER IS ON THE EYE AXIS, AND THE COMPOSITE HIDES IT COMPLETELY. No rating axis has a
  CI-clear slope on the composite (marginal or conditional) — but that is a CANCELLATION between
  channels, not an absence of error: on EYE, 4 of 6 channels carry CI-clear, opposite-signed
  contributions (K -0.000033, BABIP +0.000025, HR +0.000019) that sum to
  +0.000006. Only the exact channel decomposition exposes it.

  WHICH CHANNEL CARRIES THE LARGEST RATING-CORRELATED BIAS: the STRIKEOUT channel, against EYE —
  a rating that has
  NO PATHWAY into it (hit.k is fit on AvoidK alone; no hitter channel carries an aux term). The
  model over-predicts K by +2.01/600 per SD of EYE marginally and +2.15/600 CONDITIONAL on the
  other four ratings — it strengthens under conditioning. r = 0.525, the largest of all 24
  structurally-free cells, and it clears a family-wise permutation null over that whole family
  (p = 0.000). The BABIP channel carries a second, independent EYE error (conditional
  +1.12 pts×1000 per SD, CI-clear), and the BIP-chain coupling check shows only ~6% of it is
  the mechanical echo of the K error — two errors, not one seen twice.
  IT IS A PROPERTY OF THE FORM, NOT OF THE 42-43 FIT: the K-vs-EYE slope is CI-clear in 7 of 7
  individual seasons (5 of them OUT of the fit window) at +2.13/600 per SD pooled over
  2037-2043 [+1.65, +2.67]. (Not 7 independent replications — the same card pool
  recurs — but the same defect every time, in and out of frame.)

  WHICH AXES ARE GENUINELY INDEPENDENT: EYE, and only EYE. It is the sole rating whose slope
  survives holding the other four fixed (in the K, BABIP and HBP channels). POW, AvoidK, the BABIP
  rating and GAP produce nothing that survives conditioning. Notably HR-vs-EYE is CI-clear
  MARGINALLY and dies conditionally — the single-axis trap, firing exactly where the doctrine says
  it would.

  SHAPE OR ADDITIVE: ADDITIVE. Every 2-D corner tested inside its permutation null
  (POW×EYE p=0.883, POW×GAP p=0.902, EYE×AvoidK p=0.998). One M3 product term (POW×EYE) is
  CI-clear, but it is a single uncorrected df against a multiplicity-safe null that did not fire —
  NOT ESTABLISHED, flagged for a larger sample.

  THE ELITE-POWER TAIL DOES NOT REPRODUCE IN FRAME, AND THAT IS THE EXPECTED RESULT. The line
  measured is the RAW deployed event line; the deployed hitter-tail correction is the EXACT

  Sources: fitHitForm (src/training/forms.ts:211) — what each curve is FIT on; predictHitting
  (src/model/raw-poly.ts:27) — what each curve is EVALUATED on; the deployed artifact's own
  fitted curves (printed below, so this is the DEPLOYED wiring, not the form template's).

  channel          deployed curve                 PRIMARY rating AUX terms
  ---------------- ------------------------------ -------------- ------------------------------
  hit.bb  (uBB)    log                            EYE            NONE
  hit.k   (K)      log                            AvoidK         NONE
  hit.hr  (HR)     rawpoly-2                      POW            NONE
  hit.h   (nHH)    rating log + BIP log           BABIP-rating   the derived BIP count (its OWN fitted curve)
  hit.xbh (share)  log                            GAP            NONE

  ⇒ THE HEADLINE STRUCTURAL FACT, and it is the OPPOSITE of the pitcher side: NOT ONE HITTER
  CHANNEL CARRIES AN AUX TERM. fitHitForm never calls fitEventAux (compare fitPitForm, which calls
  it for pit.bb and pit.hr under `stuffAug`), and the deployed artifact confirms it — aux present
  on hit.*: NONE. Every hitter channel is a ONE-RATING channel.

  WHICH RATINGS CANNOT REACH WHICH CHANNELS (the "nowhere to land" analysis):
    · EYE, AvoidK, POW  →  reach hit.h ONLY through the BIP COUNT (BIP = 600 − BB − K − HR − 5),
      and BIP enters hit.h's design as its own fitted ln(BIP) term ⇒ the hits residual is
      orthogonalized against the BIP COMBINATION, but NOT against eye/kRat/pow individually.
    · BABIP-rating  →  NO pathway into BB, K or HR. None. It touches only the hit RATE.
    · GAP           →  NO pathway into BB, K, HR, BIP, or the hit TOTAL. It splits hits into 1B vs
      XBH and nothing else. A GAP-correlated error can therefore ONLY surface in the XBH-share
      channel (and, through the wOBA weights, in the composite).
    · POW           →  NO pathway into the contact rate, the walk rate, or the K rate.

  HOW TO READ EVERY RESIDUAL BELOW (the least-squares orthogonality caveat, hitter version):
  each channel was fit BY WEIGHTED LEAST SQUARES on its own primary rating, so IN FRAME its
  residual is pushed toward zero slope ON THAT RATING BY CONSTRUCTION — but is STRUCTURALLY FREE
  on the other four. So:
    · a channel showing a slope on a FOREIGN rating (e.g. HR residual vs GAP) is the informative
      case: the form has no way to express it;
    · a channel showing a slope on its OWN rating is the suppressed case — for the LOG channels
      (bb/k/h/xbh) the fit absorbs level and linear-in-ln, and for hit.hr (raw-QUAD in POW) it
      absorbs level, linear AND quadratic in raw POW. In both, only the CUBIC partial is free.
  THREE REASONS THE ORTHOGONALITY IS ONLY APPROXIMATE, not exact (so a small own-rating slope is
  not automatically a finding): (a) the fit weights PA^0.75, this artifact weights PA; (b) the fit
  target for hit.h/hit.xbh is a CHAIN quantity (predicted-BIP, predicted-hits) while the residual
  here is measured against the OBSERVED line; (c) the deployed hit.hr was NOT vertex-pinned
  (vertexPinned lists only pit.hr) but IS tangent-linear-extended beyond its fit domain — see Part 7.

  ARE THESE ONE ERROR OR SEVERAL? THE BIP-CHAIN COUPLING CHECK. A K mis-prediction moves the
  predicted BIP count, and hit.h reads BIP through its own fitted ln(BIP) term — so a K error
  MECHANICALLY produces a BABIP-rate error. If the BABIP cell were merely that echo it would
  not be a separate finding. The arithmetic (all at the pooled means):
    fitted ln(BIP) coefficient 119.47;  mean predicted nHH 125.1/600, mean predicted BIP 415.4/600
    ⇒ H-on-BIP elasticity 0.955 at the mean, and d(babip)/d(BIP) = (b_BIP − nHH)/BIP² = -3.27e-5
    measured ΔK slope on EYE: +2.006 K/600 per SD ⇒ PREDICTED BABIP echo +0.066 pts×1000 per SD
    MEASURED BABIP slope on EYE: +1.068 pts×1000 per SD
    ⇒ the chain echo explains 6% of it. The BABIP cell is therefore NOT mostly a mechanical
    echo of the K cell — it is a largely SEPARATE contact-rate error on the same rating axis.
    (Note the elasticity is BELOW 1, so a K over-prediction shrinks predicted hits LESS than
    proportionally — which is why the echo has the sign it does.)

### VERDICT
################################################################################

  ── THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER ──

  THE LARGEST BIAS IS A LEVEL, NOT A SLOPE, AND IT IS ANCHOR-ABSORBABLE: +0.00105 of the
  +0.00111 composite (+94%) is the HBP channel — the model assigns every hitter a
  FIXED 6/600 hit-by-pitch against an observed mean of 5.15/600. A pure constant is exactly what a
  per-role anchor CAN absorb, so it is not a per-card error and it is not the interesting result.
  ON THE COMPOSITE, NO RATING AXIS IS A CI-CLEAR DRIVER — marginally none of the five, conditionally
  none of the five. BUT THAT COMPOSITE NULL IS A CANCELLATION, NOT AN ABSENCE, and the exact
  decomposition is what exposes it: on the EYE axis 4 of 6 channels carry CI-clear slopes that
  very nearly annihilate each other (K -0.000033, BABIP +0.000025, HR +0.000019 — sum ≈ the
  +0.000006 composite). A composite-only read would have reported "nothing here".

  ── THE FINDING: EYE REACHES CHANNELS THE FORM DENIES IT ──

  The strongest, most robust result in this artifact is a STRUCTURALLY-FREE cell (Part 1b): the
  strikeout channel's residual against EYE.
    · K residual vs EYE: marginal +2.006 K/600 per SD [+1.475, +2.566] ★,
      CONDITIONAL on all four other ratings +2.150 [+1.471, +2.928] ★ — it does not merely
      survive conditioning, it STRENGTHENS. Weighted correlation r = 0.525, the largest of all
      24 free cells, and it clears a FAMILY-WISE permutation null over the whole family (p = 0.000),
      so it is not a multiple-comparisons artifact.
      MEANING: hit.k is fit on AvoidK ALONE and EYE has NO pathway into it (Part 0). The model
      OVER-predicts strikeouts for high-EYE hitters by ~2.1 K/600 per SD of EYE and under-predicts
      them for low-EYE hitters — a monotone quintile ramp (-3.08 → +3.33/600). Plate
      discipline genuinely suppresses strikeouts beyond what the contact rating expresses, and
      the deployed hitter form CANNOT express it. This is the exact hitter analogue of the
      pitcher form's stuffAug legs — a second rating that genuinely reaches a channel.
    · BABIP residual vs EYE: marginal +1.068 pts×1000 per SD [+0.330, +1.844] ★,
      CONDITIONAL +1.117 [+0.174, +2.050] ★ — also survives. AND IT IS NOT THE FIRST
      FINDING'S ECHO: the BIP-chain coupling check (Part 1b) shows the mechanical echo of the K
      error through the fitted ln(BIP) term accounts for only ~6% of it. Two separate errors on
      one axis, not one error seen twice.
    · HR residual vs EYE is CI-clear MARGINALLY but its conditional CI covers zero ⇒ that third
      cell is a proxy for the correlated ratings (EYE×POW r +0.299), NOT an independent axis.
      Reporting it as a finding would be exactly the single-axis trap the doctrine warns about.
    · The HBP channel ALSO carries a CI-clear EYE slope (conditional +0.000098/SD ★): the fixed
      6/600 constant has no rating pathway at all, so the real EYE-correlation of hit-by-pitches lands
      entirely in the residual. Small, but a rating-correlated bias an anchor cannot absorb.

  ── SHAPE OR ADDITIVE ──

  ADDITIVE. No tested 2-D corner came close to its permutation null: POW×EYE p=0.883, POW×GAP p=0.902, EYE×AvoidK p=0.998.
  ONE TENSION, FLAGGED NOT HIDDEN: the POW×EYE product term in the M3 regression IS
  CI-clear (+0.000736 [+0.000023, +0.001795]) while the corresponding grid null
  says p=0.883. These are DIFFERENT tests: the M3 term is a single pre-specified degree of
  freedom with no multiplicity correction, the grid null is a MAX statistic over 9 cells (i.e.
  already multiplicity-corrected). The multiplicity-safe test does not fire, the CI barely
  excludes zero, and the meta-model's interaction coefficients (Part 9) are untested ridge point
  estimates. CALL: a shape corner is NOT ESTABLISHED. It is a pre-registerable question for a
  larger sample, not a result.

  ── THE ELITE-POWER TAIL (the standing published residual) ──

  LINE MEASURED: the RAW deployed event line, with the hitter-tail correction ABSENT — not by
  choice but by construction (zero pool gap ⇒ every λ·w(g) = 0 ⇒ applyHitTail is the exact
  identity on league data). SPLIT THE CLAIM IN TWO:
    · the LEVEL claim (elite POW under-predicted) does NOT reproduce in frame — the top POW
      quartile's HR residual is +0.231/600, i.e. very slightly OVER-predicted, the OPPOSITE
      sign; the mid quartile is -0.307/600.
    · the SPREAD claim (elite calibration slope above pooled) is DIRECTIONALLY present but weak:
      elite 1.528 vs pooled 0.997, and the elite CI covers 1.0. Published out-of-frame: 2.44 vs 1.17.
  NEITHER IS EVIDENCE AGAINST THE PUBLISHED RESIDUAL, and the artifact should not be read that
  way. In frame is the wrong frame for it: the correction is switched off here by design, the
  hit-tail doc itself records league in-frame as calibrated, and — the sharper structural point —
  0 of 173 in-frame rows sit beyond the hit.hr quad's fit-domain edge (z 2.40, POW 172),
  which is exactly where the late bend the correction fights would live. THE LEAGUE FRAME DOES
  NOT EXERCISE THE FAILURE MODE. Confirming or refuting the tail requires the out-of-frame
  instrument (the cwhit sample), which is out of scope here.

  ── SECONDARY: CURVE-SHAPE MISFIT ON OWN RATINGS (2 of the 4 log channels) ──

  On an own-rating cell the fit absorbs level and linear-in-ln, so ONLY the cubic partial is free
  — and it is CI-clear in 2 of the four log channels:
    · hit.k (log on AvoidK)              cubic +0.2487 [+0.0050, +0.6386] ★  (K/600 per SD)
    · hit.h (log on the BABIP rating)    cubic -0.2727 [-0.5573, -0.0990] ★  (BABIP pts×1000 per SD)
  These are genuine functional-form misfits of the LOG family on its own driver (a log curve
  cannot bend that way), not rating-shape effects. They are an order less consequential than
  the EYE finding in wOBA terms and are recorded, not acted on.

  ── FRAME NOTE (Part 8) ──

  The composite LEVEL moves monotonically across seasons: 2037 -0.0066  2038 -0.0066  2039 -0.0028  2040 -0.0024  2041 -0.0026  2042 +0.0016  2043 +0.0006.
  Out-of-frame seasons sit NEGATIVE (the model under-credits) and in-frame seasons positive. That
  is the league frame strengthening every window — cards improve each season, so a model fit on
  42-43 opposition scores earlier, weaker-opposition seasons low. It is the NULL, not a defect,
  and it is why the all-seasons pooled conditional (which shows a CI-clear BABIP-rating slope) is
  reported as a LEAD ONLY: any rating correlated with the season composition will pick up the
  drift. Do not carry that pooled coefficient forward as a finding.

  WHAT IS AN ARTIFACT OF STRUCTURE, NOT A MECHANISM. Every hitter channel is a ONE-RATING channel
  with NO aux term (Part 0) — the exact opposite of the pitcher form, where three of four channels
  carried a ln(Stuff) aux and the contact channel had no Stuff pathway at all. Consequences:
    (a) each channel's residual is least-squares-suppressed on its OWN rating, so an own-rating
        null is uninformative — only the CUBIC partial is free there;
    (b) EVERY cross-rating cell of the Part-4 matrix is structurally free, so a cross cell that
        comes back near zero is REAL evidence of absence, not a suppression artifact;
    (c) there is no hitter analogue of the pitcher's "residual had nowhere to land except contact"
        artifact, because no hitter channel is privileged by an aux term. A cross-rating hitter
        finding therefore does NOT carry the pitcher artifact's structural discount — and the EYE
        result above sits in free cells, so it is the real thing rather than the mirror image of
        the pitcher artifact's cautionary tale.
  NAMED AS ARTIFACTS, NOT MECHANISMS:
    · the HBP LEVEL term (+94% of the composite level) — a fixed constant, absorbable by any
      per-role anchor; only its EYE SLOPE is a genuine per-card error;
    · every near-zero own-rating linear partial (HR vs POW +0.019, BB vs EYE, K vs AvoidK, …) —
      FORCED by least squares, and NOT evidence that those curves are correctly shaped;
    · the HR-vs-EYE marginal cell — CI-clear marginally, gone conditionally ⇒ a proxy for POW;
    · the pooled all-seasons BABIP-rating coefficient — confounded with the season level drift;
    · the POW×EYE M3 interaction — single untested df against a multiplicity-safe null that did
      not fire.

  MEASUREMENT ONLY. Nothing here was fit, wired, or persisted; no default changed. Any remedy is a
  separate, pre-registered step.
