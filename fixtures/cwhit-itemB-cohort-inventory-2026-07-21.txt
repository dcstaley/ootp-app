║  0. HEADLINE — THE FOUR NEW FINDINGS, IN ORDER OF CONSEQUENCE                                ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  F1. THE FIELD-SELECTION BASIS IS **NOT** ENVIRONMENT-FREE FOR HITTERS. It carries the era.
      `assembleRawHittingWoba` (src/scoring-core/woba.ts:43-49) adds `baserunningWoba(...)`, which
      reads adv_speed / adv_run / adv_steal / adv_stealRate / adv_stealInt (woba.ts:35-38), and
      `resolveCoeffs` SCALES all five by the era's runVal and sbFreq
      (src/config/coeff-resolve.ts:136-141). Therefore the era is a live term in WHICH CARDS WIN
      THE TOP-50 HITTER FIELD. The pitcher leg is genuinely env-free (assembleRawPitchingWoba,
      woba.ts:51-55, reads wOBA weights + adv_hbp only — both model-scoped). MEASURED, §4.
      ⇒ `referenceFieldStats`' cache key `${activeModelId}|${catalogSource}` (server.ts:282) is
        stale-by-construction for the hitter half. Same for `leagueExposureBaseline` (server.ts:786).

  F2. A THIRD INCONSISTENT PAIR, structurally identical to the train-vs-pool one, in the PLATOON
      EXPOSURE path: `deployment = logit(realized) − logit(leagueBaseline)` (server.ts:818) where
      `realized` comes from `computePlatoon(obs)` over VARIANT-INCLUSIVE obs (server.ts:1447 +
      1531, includeVariants defaults true at 1429) and `leagueBaseline` is
      `catalog.cards.filter(isBaseCard)` — VARIANT-FREE (server.ts:788). One subtraction, two
      populations. Nobody has looked at this one; item A did not cover it.

  F3. THE FRAME GAP HAS **TWO MORE** LEG MISMATCHES BESIDES THE VARIANT ONE.
      `buildFrameShift(trainingMeans, poolField)` subtracts legs that also differ in:
        (a) USAGE FILTER — the train leg requires maxPA ≥ minPA (default 1000) for hitters and
            maxBF ≥ minPA for pitchers (server.ts:1513-1514, 1428). The pool leg has NO usage
            filter at all (buildEligiblePool, src/config/eligibility.ts:87-97 — `ownedOnly`
            defaults false and there is no PA concept in the catalog).
        (b) ERA OF SELECTION — the train leg was selected under era-2010 + a NEUTRAL park
            (server.ts:1496-1497), frozen into the artifact. The pool leg is selected under the
            TOURNAMENT's era. By F1 that is a real difference for the hitter channels — and the
            two shipped ramps read PITCHER channels, which are HITTER-field gaps under the §10.2
            crossing (pool-stats.ts:143-148). Measured effect on the ramp input: §4.

  F4. FIELD_N AND ANCHOR_N ARE **NOT** THE SAME KIND OF OBJECT AND THE SHARED 50 IS COINCIDENTAL.
      No quantity anywhere compares a FIELD_N cohort to an ANCHOR_N cohort — every gap has
      FIELD_N on both legs, and the anchor is compared only to the constants TARGET_WOBA /
      TARGET_BASIC. They do not even rank on the same basis (§5). Moving them apart is safe in
      one direction and dangerous in the other, and the asymmetry is large.

   HOW MANY          50                                      50
   VARIANT POLICY    excludes v5                             excludes v5   (the one axis they agree on)
   CLASS             FIELD for B/D, BASELINE for A           BASELINE, always — a CONVENTION
   ITS JOB           estimate the population a card MEETS,   set the readable SCALE (0.320 / 100)
                     to form a GAP against another cohort    and the cap optimiser's budget unit

  IS THE SHARED VALUE LOAD-BEARING? **NO — it is coincidental.** Nothing computes a difference,
  ratio or gap between a FIELD_N cohort and an ANCHOR_N cohort. Every gap on the path has FIELD_N
  on BOTH legs (A vs B; C vs B; and the hit-tail ratio A vs B). The anchor's only counterparty is
  a constant (TARGET_WOBA 0.320, calibrate.ts:25; TARGET_BASIC 100, calibrate.ts:26). The two
  cohorts COMPOSE — the anchor is computed on events already re-based by a transform built from
  FIELD_N fields — but composition is not comparison, and composition does not require equal N.

  WHAT BREAKS IF ONE MOVES AND THE OTHER DOES NOT — the asymmetry is large:

   MOVING **FIELD_N** ALONE — DANGEROUS, and the danger is not where it looks.
     moves immediately: A and B field μ/σ ⇒ the PoolTransform mean-scalars (pool-stats.ts:115-121)
     ⇒ every re-based rating ⇒ every wOBA score; the frame gaps (130-150) ⇒ s_K and s_HR
     (pool-transform.ts:233, 263); the D pivots K̄/HR̄/BABIP̄ (209-219); computeHitTail's three gaps
     (hit-tail.ts:150-154) ⇒ the hitter tail strengths; and, downstream of all of that, the anchor
     and therefore every displayed number.
     ALSO INVALIDATES FITTED CONSTANTS: K_SPREAD_PIT {A 5.0871, G 152.5} and PIT_SPREAD_HR
     {A 0.2648, G 5.8} were fit AT gaps measured with FIELD_N=50; PINNED_HIT_TAIL's λs likewise.
     THE REAL TRAP: **FIELD_N IS BAKED INTO EVERY SAVED MODEL ARTIFACT.** trainingMeans is a
     top-FIELD_N field computed at TRAIN time (server.ts:1513-1514). Change FIELD_N and every
     pre-existing artifact's trainingMeans is a top-50 mean being subtracted from a top-K pool
     mean — the matched-legs property that server.ts:1478-1483 was written to establish silently
     un-does itself for every model trained before the change. No error, no warning, and the
     symptom is a gap that has drifted for no visible reason.

   MOVING **ANCHOR_N** ALONE — CONTAINED.
     moves: hitScaleVR/VL, pitchScale (calibrate.ts:113-115) and the basic anchors (166-168).
     Those multiply the assembled composite only. Every card's score moves by ONE common factor
     per (role, side); no RANKING changes, no gap changes, no ramp input changes, no fitted
     constant depends on it, and nothing is frozen into any artifact. Downstream it shifts the
     cap/slots budget unit (values are signed distances from TARGET_WOBA, calibrate.ts:142-144),
     so cap-mode roster composition can move — that is the one non-cosmetic consequence.

  ⇒ RECOMMENDATION: none, per the brief. The finding is that the pin in
    tests/one-copy-constants.test.ts:51-57 ("a SEPARATE constant that merely shares the value") is
    CORRECT, and that the asymmetry above is the reason it matters: collapsing them would couple a
    contained scale knob to a constant that is frozen inside every trained artifact.

  6.1  ORDER-DEPENDENT REFERENCE FIELD (the F1 consequence, ranked first).
       `refFieldCache` (server.ts:280-287) and `leagueBaselineCache` (server.ts:784-791) are both
       keyed `${activeModelId}|${catalogSource}` and both cleared only by `invalidateDerivedCaches`
       (server.ts:471-476), which fires on catalog refresh and model (de)activation — NOT on a
       tournament era edit (server.ts:2243 deletes only that tournament's score cache) and NOT on
       tournament switch. Since the hitter selection carries the era (§4), THE REFERENCE FIELD A
       TOURNAMENT IS SCORED AGAINST DEPENDS ON WHICH TOURNAMENT WAS SCORED FIRST AFTER THE LAST
       INVALIDATION. Same process, same config, different request order ⇒ different hitter scores.
       That is a determinism defect, independent of its (small) magnitude. It reaches: the
       PoolTransform hit affines, computeHitTail's gPow/gBab/gK, the /api/tournament/scorecard
       own-gap column (server.ts:1985), and the exposure deployment shift.

  6.2  AN UNSAVED DRAFT CAN SEED THE PROCESS-GLOBAL REFERENCE FIELD.
       `/api/position-metrics` (server.ts:2270-2281) scores a merged draft at 2274. If the ref
       cache is cold, THAT DRAFT'S ERA selects the reference field for every subsequent
       tournament. The draft is never saved and leaves no trace. Strictly a consequence of 6.1,
       but it is the most surprising path into it.

  6.3  `refDCache` IS NOT KEYED BY ACCOUNT (server.ts:873-887). Self-documented at 873-874: the
       .500 win-expectancy anchor is "computed from the FIRST request's pool — which may be
       owned-scoped by the active account", yet the cache key is the tournament id alone. Switching
       the active account does not invalidate it. Same class as 6.1 (order + shared state), already
       known to the author, recorded here for completeness.

  6.4  THE ONE-COPY PIN HAS A BLIND SPOT FOR LITERALS IN DEFAULTS.
       `tests/one-copy-constants.test.ts:32-35` matches `(export )?(const|let|var) NAME =`. It
       therefore cannot see `opts.fieldN ?? 50` / `opts.topX ?? 100` (src/eval/consistency.ts:141-142),
       which are exactly the class of second definition ruling (d) was about — equal today,
       silently divergent tomorrow. Production is unaffected only because server.ts:1886 passes
       FIELD_N explicitly.

  6.5  THE TRAIN LEG'S TWO CHANNELS COME FROM TWO POPULATIONS (server.ts:1513-1514): the hit field
       is `maxPA ≥ minPA`, the pit field is `maxBF ≥ minPA`. The pool side (B) produces both from
       ONE cohort (pool-stats.ts:98). Each gap channel is still leg-matched pairwise, so this is
       not a subtraction defect — but "the training field" is two different fields, and the
       inventory should say so rather than let the singular noun imply one.

  6.6  `tmCoeffs` READS `tournaments[0].softcaps` (server.ts:1492, 1496-1497) — i.e. whichever
       tournament the repository happens to list first decides the softcaps the model's own frame
       is selected under. Benign TODAY, and only because softcaps provably do not reach the
       selection basis (§4). It is a live tripwire under any change that puts softcaps back on the
       raw-wOBA path. The hardcoded `era-2010` + neutral park in the same expression is the F3b
       mismatch and is deliberate (documented at 1483-1485) — but the DOCUMENTED REASON ("the
       top-50 SELECTION is env/softcap-independent") is the claim §4 partially refutes.

  6.7  DEAD PER-ERA DATA THAT LOOKS LIVE: every era file carries an `hbp` factor (e.g.
       data/eras/era-1920.json `"hbp": 0.778802`), but `eraFactors` (coeff-resolve.ts:29-32) never

       `extras` (coeff-resolve.ts:120). Nobody should assume HBP is era-scaled; today it is not.

  6.8  ONE ROLE-GATED COHORT ON AN OTHERWISE ROLE-AGNOSTIC PATH: the hit-tail moment population

  6.9  UNVERIFIED, stated as such: whether the era-driven top-50 membership swaps in §4c change

# READING

1. THE VARIANT POLICY IS STILL THE BIG ONE. Nothing here displaces item A. The three new
   mismatches (F2, F3a, F3b) and the cache defect (F1) are all one to two orders of
   magnitude smaller than the −4.6 to −10.5 gap points item A sized. Prioritise accordingly.

2. BUT THE INVENTORY DID ITS JOB: it found that "same variant policy on both sides" is not
   the whole invariant. The frame gap's two legs differ on THREE axes, not one — variants,
   usage qualification, and the era the selection was performed under. Any repair that fixes
   only the variant axis will leave a subtraction whose legs still come from different
   populations, and will do so with the appearance of having been fixed.

3. THE EXPOSURE PATH HAS THE SAME DEFECT AS THE FRAME PATH AND NOBODY HAD LOOKED (F2).
   `deployment = logit(realized) − logit(leagueBaseline)` subtracts a variant-INCLUSIVE
   realized measurement from a variant-FREE league field. It is the identical shape to the
   train-vs-pool defect, in a different subsystem, and it should be repaired under the same
   ruling rather than discovered separately later.

4. "ENV-FREE SELECTION" IS TRUE FOR PITCHERS AND FALSE FOR HITTERS. The pitcher zero-row in
   §4b is exact and is the control that makes the hitter row readable. The channel is
   baserunning: era.runVal and era.sbFreq scale five adv_* coefficients that sit inside the
   hitter's raw wOBA and therefore inside the field's sort key. The cached reference field is
   consequently order-dependent (6.1) and can be seeded by an unsaved editor draft (6.2).

5. THE ANSWER TO QUESTION 2 IS CLEAN AND NEGATIVE. topHitters/topPitchers reach the optimizer
   pool, the position-rank thresholds and the display, and stop there. All three scaling-cohort
   sizes are module constants (FIELD_N, ANCHOR_N, EXPOSURE_N) and no call site reads `t.*`.
   There is no scoring input hiding in that UI field. Nothing to separate.

6. FIELD_N AND ANCHOR_N SHARE ONLY THE NUMBER. Different population, different ranking
   (the anchor deliberately zeroes baserunning; the field does not), different class, no
   comparison between them anywhere. The shared 50 is coincidental. The asymmetry that
   matters: ANCHOR_N is a contained scale knob, while FIELD_N is FROZEN INTO EVERY TRAINED
   ARTIFACT via trainingMeans — moving it silently un-matches the frame gap's legs for every
   model trained before the change.

7. WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO. It decides nothing, changes nothing, fits nothing, and moves no
   default or constant. It is the four facts per cohort, written down for the first time,
   plus the pairs those facts show to disagree.
