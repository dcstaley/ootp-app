§1  THE PARITY FIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) PINNING. src/training/forms.ts:630-636 — hitFormModel/pitFormModel now take a
    `pinned` flag (DEFAULT TRUE) and call fitHitForm(form, train, 0.75, pinned ? [] : undefined)
    / fitPitForm(form, train, 0.75, pinned ? [] : undefined). Production
    (src/server/server.ts:1567) passes a `vertexPinned` collector to both fits, so any
    degree-2 rawpoly channel whose UNCONSTRAINED vertex lands strictly inside the fit domain
    is refit with the vertex pinned at the domain max (pinQuadAtDomainMax).
    The pins are DATA-DEPENDENT — computed per fit by inDomainVertex, never a fixed list —
    so parity means handing every fit its own FRESH collector, exactly as saveTrainedModel
    does. That is what the wrappers now do (a new [] per fit call, including per CV fold).
    The collector is discarded by the harness (nothing downstream reads the provenance);
    §3 reports which channels actually pinned.

(b) CANDIDATES. src/training/forms.ts — added to FORM_ENTRIES: PARETO_PIT (the DEPLOYED
    pitcher form since 2026-07-14, previously not a candidate at all), STUFFAUG_PIT (the
    form it replaced), SATBB_PIT, and a NEW form PARETO_NOAUG_PIT ('woba·pareto-noaug') =
    PARETO_PIT with stuffAug:false, so the aux is a testable candidate instead of baked in.

(c) HARNESS SCOPE. src/training/evaluate.ts — EvalOpts gained an optional `years` filter so a
    run can restrict the year universe (window + both OOT blocks). Omitting it reproduces the
    previous behavior exactly. Used here to keep 'League Files/Old Data' (2032–33) out.

Q1 — DOES THE DEPLOYED CONFIGURATION (RAWPOLY_HIT + PARETO_PIT, pinned) WIN?

No — and neither does anything else. On the two metrics that carry the deliverable (top-26
overlap and value-regret) the field is statistically indistinguishable. Across the four
out-of-time comparisons in §7, spanning both directions and both roles, NOT ONE rival's
Δtop26 or Δregret confidence interval excludes zero against the deployed form. The typical
95% interval on Δtop26 is ±0.077 to ±0.115 — that is 2 to 3 roster slots out of 26 — because
only 63–82 distinct CARDS clear the 1000-PA/BF bar in any test block. The ordinal ranks in §5
(where woba·pareto lands 17/20 on one in-sample cell and 2/20 on another) are noise at this
resolution and should not be read as a result. That is the primary finding: the bake-off
cannot separate model FORMS on deliverable outcomes with this much league data. It could not
have done so before the parity fix either; the fix means the comparison is now at least
scoring the right model.

On weighted Pearson — which does separate, being a pooled-fidelity statistic rather than a
26-slot cut — the deployed forms hold up:
  · HITTER woba·rawpoly is CI-clear BETTER than woba·rawlin in 4 of 4 directions (−0.019 to
    −0.034) and than woba·qpow-lin in 3 of 4 (−0.011 to −0.021), and is a statistical TWIN of
    woba·rawcubic (Δ from −0.0001 to +0.0020, every CI spanning 0). rawcubic is not a reason to
    switch: indistinguishable, and it trips the monotone gate on HR and XBH in three of the
    four in-sample windows. woba·perbip is the one form that genuinely SPLITS the directions —
    CI-clear better forward on 40+41→42+43 (+0.0093) and CI-clear worse backward on
    42+43→37-41 (−0.0031). Both effects are ≤0.01 Pearson and neither shows up in top26 or
    regret, so this is a fidelity trade, not a deliverable one.
  · PITCHER woba·pareto is CI-clear better than woba·rawlin in 1 of 4 directions and than its
    own no-aux variant in 2 of 4, and is statistically tied with woba·stuffaug, woba·satbb,
    woba·perbip and woba·matchupK in every direction.
Conclusion: keep the deployed configuration. It is never beaten at measurable confidence, it
beats the low-flexibility forms on fidelity, and the forms that match it are either gate-dirty
(rawcubic) or already-known equivalents. But the honest framing is 'not refuted', not 'wins'.

Q2 — DOES stuffAug EARN ITS PLACE?

Partly — on fidelity yes, on the deliverable it is unmeasurable. The direction is consistent:
ΔwPearson (pareto − pareto-noaug) is POSITIVE in all six evaluations in §6 (+0.012, +0.020,
+0.017, +0.023, +0.032, +0.033), and the cid-clustered CI excludes zero in FOUR of the six
(both CV windows, the 37+38→39-43 forward, and the 40+41→37-39 backward). Six of six
same-signed is itself informative beyond the individual intervals: under a true null the
chance of that is 1/32, and these six evaluations are not independent but they are not
nested either. So the aux is doing real work in the fit.
But in deliverable terms it is a null: Δtop26 runs −0.030 to +0.016 with every CI spanning
zero, and Δregret runs −0.00021 to +0.00075 wOBA per slot — five of the six magnitudes under
0.00022 — again with every CI spanning zero. At 63–82 clusters the design simply cannot
resolve an effect of that size on a 26-slot cut.
Recommendation: KEEP the aux. It is consistently positive on fidelity, never negative on the
deliverable, and the sequential-resolution mechanism (high Stuff getting ahead in counts
suppressing walks) is coherent. This run is its first committed evidence artifact — the
previous position was that it had none. Two caveats on the record: (i) on the [2042,2043]
window the aux changes WHETHER pit.hr pins (§3), so that arm is not a strict one-term contrast
— both arms are nonetheless fitted exactly as production fits them; (ii) the fidelity gain is
small enough that it should not be cited as decisive for anything downstream.
