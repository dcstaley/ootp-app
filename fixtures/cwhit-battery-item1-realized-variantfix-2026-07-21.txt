# READING — the correction changes the numbers, not the verdict
################################################################################

WHAT WAS WRONG. The realized weighting joined observed rows to the catalog on
Name+VAL+Hand and kept only observed VLvl == 0. Variants are generated at runtime and
absent from the catalog CSV, so every v5 row was unmatchable and was dropped. The
coverage table above measures what that cost: v5 rows carry 14.8-28.6% of each cell's
realized opposition usage, so the "field actually faced" was reconstructed with a
fifth to a quarter of it missing — and missing non-randomly, since a v5 row is a
strictly better card than its base.

Weights now come from the program's ONE sample builder via Rec.cid (CardID|VLvl), so
base and v5 are separate opponents with separate ratings and separate usage. Orphan
count is 0 in all ten cells: every joined observed row has a card in the opponent set.

1. THE K MISS IS UNCHANGED AND IS NOW MEASURED ON THE RIGHT FIELD. The candidate stays
   flat at 1.087/1.084/1.082/1.074/1.040 against needs of 1.04-1.82. Gold sits at 1.074
   against 1.78. Three weightings of the opposing field — uniform, realized-without-
   variants, realized-with-variants — move the K candidate by less than 0.01 anywhere.
   The battery's central finding survives the correction intact: whatever drives the K
   need, it is not the composition of the opposing field.

2. THE CHANNEL-SELECTIVE PATTERN STRENGTHENS. Fixing the weights improves HR by a
   further 11% (0.543 -> 0.485) and BABIP slightly (0.188 -> 0.180) while K moves
   0.002 the wrong way. The same perturbation that two channels respond to, the third
   ignores. That contrast is the finding, and it now rests on a correctly weighted field.

3. HR STILL SLOPES THE WRONG WAY. Variant-corrected 2.100 -> 1.262, monotone falling,
   against a need measured as flat-to-rising ~1.19. Better in level at every tier, wrong
   in shape. The weighting error was real but was not the whole HR story.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO. It does not make the realized weighting shippable — realized
usage is not ex-ante computable, so this remains a diagnostic. The production candidate
is still the eligible-side coordinate, and the eligible side is deliberately left
non-variant because it mirrors what production computes (basePool sets the distribution;
variants are scored but do not set it).

JOIN NOTE. The corrected base-level counts run 1-3 cards BELOW the old private join in
every cell (e.g. iron hit 254 vs 256). The builder disambiguates name collisions on an
event-space fingerprint and refuses ambiguous pairs; name equality accepted some of them.
The old run also reported 1-2 unmatched observed keys and a few duplicate-key drops per
cell, which the cid join does not produce at all.
