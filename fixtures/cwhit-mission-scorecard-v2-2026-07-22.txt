╔═══ B. THE DUEL — per format x role x channel, LEVEL and SHAPE kept SEPARATE ═══════════════════════╗
LEVEL  = mean(pred) vs mean(obs). Verdict = D|bias| (paired bootstrap): NEGATIVE means OUR bias is smaller.
         A level loss can restate a KNOWN, ACCEPTED out-of-frame gap — our level corrections live in the eval stack by design.
SHAPE  = per-card agreement AFTER DE-MEANING. Verdict = Dcorr ONLY, because corr is SCALE-FREE: it answers "who ORDERS
         the cards better", and a spread defect cannot masquerade as a discrimination defect. MAE is NOT a verdict input.
SPREAD = SD(pred)/SD(obs), raw and noise-DECONVOLVED (dcv). Reported as a diagnostic, NOT as one of the two axes.
* = 95% CI excludes 0. Duel CIs are a 2000-rep PAIRED bootstrap over cards (paired: both models are scored on the
SAME cards, so shared card difficulty cancels and the CI is on the DIFFERENCE). UNREL = noise-share CI upper >= 100%
⇒ dcv is not a measurement (the raw ratio beside it still is). THIN cells print but carry NO verdict on either axis.
