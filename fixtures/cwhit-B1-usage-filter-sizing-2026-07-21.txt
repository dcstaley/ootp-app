── 1d. THE MECHANISM — why the direction comes out the way it does ──
  The prediction in §0 reasons about a POPULATION mean: a usage floor keeps the strong and drops the
  weak, so the surviving mean rises. That reasoning is correct for a FULL-POOL mean. The train leg is
  NOT a full-pool mean — it is the TOP-50 field cut from the surviving population, and the floor is
  applied BEFORE that cut. Top-50-of-a-subset can never beat top-50-of-the-superset in the selection
  metric, so on the metric the field is SELECTED BY, the floor can only make the field WEAKER.
  It removes CANDIDATES from a competition the strong were already winning.

  (a) the SELECTION metric of the chosen field, by floor

── HEADLINE ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The pull is ONE-DIRECTIONAL, and it runs the OPPOSITE WAY to the prediction. The audit predicted
that the 1000-PA/BF floor INFLATES the training mean and so inflates the gap. Measured: the floor
DEFLATES it. Removing the floor entirely raises gap pit.vR.stu by +3.43 in every one of the nine
formats and raises the shipped K correction by 13-33% of its current magnitude; raising the floor
to 2000 lowers the gap by -1.39 and cuts the correction by 5-14%. The size of the whole axis, on the
one ruler all three mismatches share: s_K - 1 spans 86-143% of its production value, max deviation
43%. That places this axis THIRD of the three — the smallest — but only barely behind the
cohort-size axis, and not small in absolute terms.

── (1) THE SIZING ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Reproduction is exact: the floor-1000 arm reproduces the frozen artifact's trainingMeans to
+0.0000 on all nine channels (see the raw output, section 1b). The tool is therefore measuring
production's own construction, not a lookalike.

Every floor is EVALUABLE and all five are reported. The population never falls below FIELD_N = 50

── (2) THE MECHANISM, and why the predicted direction is wrong ─────────────────
The prediction reasons about a POPULATION mean, and it is correct about one: cards that clear
1000 PA/BF in a real league are strength-selected relative to the league. But the train leg is NOT a
population mean. It is the TOP-50 FIELD cut from the surviving population, and the floor is applied
BEFORE that cut. Top-50-of-a-subset can never beat top-50-of-the-superset on the metric it is
selected by, so on that metric the floor can only make the field WEAKER. Confirmed monotonically in
both roles (section 1d(a)):

    floor    hitter field mean wOBA (higher=stronger)   pitcher field mean allowed wOBA (lower=stronger)
    0                                        0.35376                                           0.31550
    250                                      0.35148                                           0.31695
    500                                      0.34969                                           0.31695
    1000                                     0.34813                                           0.31914
    2000                                     0.34388                                           0.32159

The floor is not acting as a strength filter on this path. It acts as a CANDIDATE filter, deleting
high-rated, low-usage league cards from a competition whose weak end the top-50 cut had already
removed. At the production floor, 23 of the 71 distinct members of the no-floor hitter field and 15
of the 50 members of the no-floor pitcher field are removed from the candidate set outright; only
48/65 of the production hitter field and 35/50 of the production pitcher field are shared with the
no-floor field (section 1d(b)). The audit's premise about the population is true; it is simply not a
premise about the ESTIMATOR, because the top-N cut already does the strength selection, and does it
on the model's own quality metric rather than on realized playing time.

CAUTION FOR ANY READER WHO WANTS TO GENERALISE THIS: the composite direction does NOT transfer to
individual channels. Every one of the nine channels is non-monotone in the floor somewhere (section
1c), because the composite ranking trades channels against each other, so a weaker field can be
higher on a given rating. The consequence lands directly on the ramp inputs — gap pit.vR.stu moves
+3.43 at f=0, +4.44 at f=250, +1.62 at f=500 and -1.39 at f=2000: neither monotone nor one-signed.
So the axis is one-directional in FIELD STRENGTH and NOT one-directional in any number a ramp reads.

── (5) WHAT BOUNDS ANY USE OF THESE NUMBERS ────────────────────────────────────
THE DEPLOYED trainingMeans IS FROZEN. It was computed once, at train time, at the production floor,
and is baked into data/trained-models/league-42-43.json. saveTrainedModel derives it from
body.minPA; nothing at scoring time can reach it. A FLOOR CHANGE IS THEREFORE A RETRAIN-COUPLED
DECISION, NOT A CONFIG KNOB — and a retrain re-fits eventForm, ratingEnvelope, platoon and
wobaWeights alongside it, so a floor change would never arrive alone. It would also silently
re-coordinate both shipped ramps, which were fit against the production-floor gap; the precedent is
on record (the 2026-07-21 refresh paid a +5.4% "coordinate tax" on K_SPREAD_PIT for exactly this
kind of frame move, src/model/pool-transform.ts).

RECORDED, NOT RECOMMENDED — one observation any decision would need. minPA does DOUBLE DUTY. It
gates which observations the model FITS on (server.ts:1452, hitQual / pitQual) and, separately, it
gates the population the frame cohort is cut from (server.ts:1517-1518). Those are two different
jobs sharing one number. Matching the frame legs on this axis means matching on "no usage concept",
since the pool leg has none and cannot acquire one — but that is a statement about the SECOND use
only, and does not require touching the fit floor. Nothing here decides that; it is noted because a
reader who took the -13% to -33% figure as a call to lower the floor would otherwise be proposing to
change the fit as well, which this tool did not measure.

WHAT THIS TOOL DOES NOT CLAIM: that any floor is correct. It measures the sensitivity and the
mechanism. Notably f=0 is not self-evidently better on quality grounds — its field is selected
purely on ratings over 391 league cards with no evidence any of them were ever deployed — but it IS
the arm under which both legs of the gap share one rule, which is the same consistency argument that
motivated matching the cohort SIZE at section 11.13.
