THE HEADLINE LOOKS POSITIVE AND IS CONFOUNDED. All-pairs gives HIGH-minus-LOW slope
-0.289 with boot95 [-0.639, -0.005], nominally excluding zero. Three controls say do not
believe it:

1. THE POOLED SLOPE FLIPS SIGN between the two samples: +0.059 over all pairs, -0.334 over
   env-matched pairs only. A relationship that reverses when era/park are held fixed is
   reporting the environment, not the opposition.

2. THE BB9 FALSIFICATION CONTROL MOVES AS MUCH AS K9. Pooled dBB9~dOpp slope is +0.067
   against dK9's +0.059 — BB9 should be near-inert to a change in the opposing AVOID-K
   field. Equal response means the all-pairs signal is not K-specific.

3. R-SQUARED IS ~0.002-0.02 across the all-pairs fits. The line explains essentially none
   of the variance.

THE CLEAN TEST IS NULL AND UNDERPOWERED. Env-matched pairs (N=93): HIGH-minus-LOW slope
-0.174, boot95 [-0.721, +0.153] — INCLUDES ZERO. The CI is 0.87 wide against an effect
plausibly of order 0.1-0.3, so this neither detects nor excludes interaction.

THE ROOT CAUSE IS THE LEVER, AND IT IS STRUCTURAL. Across all 14 formats the opposing
Avoid-K field mean spans only 81.36 -> 88.75, a 7.4-point range on the rating scale. Worse,
that range is almost entirely a function of the VALUE WINDOW: every format sharing a window
has an IDENTICAL opposition measure (bronzequick / bronzeheart / latebronze /
bronzecapweekly all 83.80), so those pairs contribute NO contrast at all and are dropped.
The surviving pairs are exactly the ones that also change era, park and pool size. The
opposition lever and the environment lever are the same lever in this corpus.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE ITEM-1 MISS. The pre-registered discriminator was to separate
WRONG-CURVATURE from MISSING-INTERACTION. It cannot do that here. The miss remains
ambiguous, and the ambiguity is now a measured property of the available data rather than
an open analysis step.

WHAT WOULD ACTUALLY SEPARATE THEM (not run, not proposed as a plan):
  - A contrast where opposition moves WITHOUT the value window moving. Within-format
    opponent variation across instances would do it; the per-format aggregates cannot.
  - The eligibility windows are NESTED, so a wider window is a superset. Any cross-window
    contrast therefore changes the pool's composition and its size together.
