## 5. THE FRAME-DRIFT CONFOUND, HANDLED EXPLICITLY
################################################################################

     Cards improve every season, so older years sit in a systematically WEAKER frame: the Stuff
     distribution shifts, and so does the quality of the opposing hitters. A LEVEL residual in an old
     year can therefore be frame drift rather than curve misfit, and must not be read naively.

     THE ARGUMENT, from these numbers rather than from first principles:
      • LEVEL is NOT robust. It ranges -2.63 … +0.44/600 across seasons (spread 3.07) and correlates
        +0.93 with the season's mean Stuff and +0.91 with opposing avoid-K. An old-year level residual
        is not interpretable as curve misfit — exactly the warning, confirmed in the data.
      • SHAPE (the cubic) IS robust. Removing per-year levels moves it -0.4983 → -0.4976; removing
        per-year×hand levels moves it to -0.4628. Mechanically this is expected — the cubic partial is
        orthogonal to the constant in the weighted basis, so a per-group additive shift cannot enter it
        — and the numbers confirm the mechanism rather than merely assuming it. Standardizing Stuff
        WITHIN each season, which also neutralises the drift in the Stuff DISTRIBUTION (not just its
        level), leaves -0.4752 CI-clear. And the per-year cubic tracks the frame proxies weakly
        (-0.65 vs mean Stuff, -0.49 vs opposing kRat) against LEVEL's +0.93 / +0.91.
      • The HAND CONTRAST is robust by construction and confirmed empirically: vR and vL rows come from
        the SAME season, the SAME league files and largely the SAME cards, so any year-level frame shift
        is common to both arms and differences it out. The per-year×hand FE row above is the direct
        check — it removes an arbitrary per-year-per-hand level and the cubic survives at -0.4628.
        What frame drift CANNOT be differenced away by the hand contrast is a change in the platoon
        COMPOSITION of the opposition itself; that is a separate exposure question, not this residual.

## 6. VERDICT, IN FULL
################################################################################

  (a) DOES THE CUBIC K MISFIT REPLICATE OUT-OF-SAMPLE?
      YES IN THE vR ARM (CI-clear there); POOLED ONLY DIRECTIONAL. The form was fit on 2042+2043; on 2037–2041, data it has never seen, the cubic
      partial is -0.4714 [-1.2738, +0.0990] over 497 rows / 61 cards — the same sign as the
      in-sample -0.5414, at 0.87× the magnitude, and 7/7 individual seasons carry the same
      sign. Pooled, that OOS interval grazes zero; the vR ARM does not — -0.8623 [-1.7487, -0.1836] against an
      in-sample -0.8690, i.e. the misfit reproduces at 99% of its in-sample magnitude (a 0.8% difference)
      on years the fit never saw.
      In-sample shrinkage therefore did NOT manufacture the 2026-07-23 finding. The shape survives year
      FE, year×hand FE and within-season standardization (§5), so it is not the frame-drift level
      confound wearing a curve. THE CAVEAT THAT LIMITS ALL OF THIS: the same ~70 cards populate every
      season (53/61 OOS cards are also fit-window cards), so this is replication ACROSS TIME on largely
      the SAME cards — not replication on fresh cards, which this corpus cannot supply.

  (b) IS HAND-CONDITIONING WARRANTED?
      YES. On the full 2037–2043 window the vR−vL cubic difference is -1.0668, with paired
      card-cluster CIs [-2.1075, -0.0817] (p=0.034) and [-2.0980, -0.1010] (p=0.034) under two independent
      rng streams — stable, so the reading is usable this time. Season-by-season the contrast is negative in
      7/7 years, and it is the same sign in-sample and out-of-sample, which is the
      strongest single indicator either way. The hand LEVEL gap remains UNRESOLVED (+1.06/600,
      p=0.481) and should not be carried into any proposal.

  SCOPE. Measurement only: no fit, no refit, no defaults touched, nothing wired, nothing committed.
  This EXTENDS the 2026-07-23 and 2026-07-25 artifacts; it supersedes neither.
