THE REPLICATION CHECK COMES FIRST. The right-hand column of each divergence table
recomputes the OLD VLvl-0-only convention on the new join. It reproduces the first run
to within a few thousandths at iron through gold (e.g. Eye 0.183 vs 0.180, Power 0.427
vs 0.427, BABIP 1.239 vs 1.238 at gold). Diamond moves more (Eye 0.468 vs 0.411, Gap
1.347 vs 1.279) — the thinnest cell, 124 played cards, where the stricter fingerprint
join and a couple of dropped ambiguous names matter most. So the first run's arithmetic
was sound; what was wrong with it was the field it was computed over.

1. THE ELIGIBLE-POOL ASSUMPTION IS OFF BY 0.27 TO 1.37 SD ON THE HITTER FIELD, and the
   variant correction makes it WORSE everywhere it moves it — as it must, since a v5 row
   is a strictly better card than its base and the old convention gave it weight 0. Eye
   at iron 0.18 -> 0.27, Power at gold 0.81 -> 0.94, Gap at iron 0.38 -> 0.53.

2. THE PITCHER FIELD IS OFF BY MORE, AND IT HAD NEVER BEEN MEASURED. Up to 1.62 SD
   (pHR at gold). Pools are complete environments; this is the opposition hitters face
   and it diverges from its eligible pool harder than the hitter field does.

3. ONE SIGN REVERSAL, AT IRON, ON CONTROL: -0.20 SD. The pitchers that actually get
   played at iron have WORSE control than the average eligible iron pitcher, while every
   other tier runs positive and diamond reaches +0.99. Every other channel on both sides
   is positive at every tier. Whatever selects iron pitchers into play, it is not control.

4. GOLD STILL BREAKS THE ORDER, NOW ON BOTH SIDES. Hitter field: Eye PEAKS at gold
   (0.817, above diamond's 0.490); BABIP and Gap sit BELOW silver. Pitcher field: pHR
   peaks at gold (1.624 vs diamond 1.502) while Stuff sits below silver (0.774 vs 1.075).
   Gold's realized opposition is off in a different PATTERN from its neighbours, not
   merely off by more, and the variant correction does not dissolve that.

5. COVERAGE COLLAPSES WITH WINDOW WIDTH, unchanged: the share of eligible base cards
   that ever play runs 40.9% (iron) down to 6.1% (diamond) on the pitcher side. A wider
   value window admits far more cards than it causes to be used.

6. CONCENTRATION IS SLIGHTLY LOWER THAN FIRST REPORTED once variants carry their own
   weight (Gini 0.58-0.77 against 0.62-0.80; top 10% take 42-66% against 44-69%) — the
   v5 rows split usage that the old convention either dropped or implicitly attributed.

WHAT THIS IS NOT. Realized usage is not ex-ante computable, so none of this is a
candidate coordinate. It measures the size of an assumption's error. The eligible side
is quoted exactly as production computes it (basePool, uniform, non-variant) on purpose:
it is the thing under test.
